One of the most magical times to see Wat Arun 🇹🇭✨

Come before sunset and watch the Temple of Dawn glow as the sky changes. Definitely one of those Bangkok moments you need to experience at least once.

💡 Pro tip: Arrive before golden hour so you can explore first and stay for the sunset.
