5 BANGKOK ROOFTOP BARS WITH VIEWS THAT DON’T FEEL REAL 👀🌇

Bangkok really knows how to do rooftop views. From golden hour to city lights, these are 5 rooftop bars worth saving for your next night out:

✨ Vanilla Sky Rooftop
Great for sunset, skyline views and a relaxed rooftop vibe.

🥂 CRU Champagne Bar
One of the most iconic rooftop spots in the city with a dramatic view over central Bangkok.

🌅 Paradise Lost
Colorful, stylish and perfect if you want a rooftop that feels a little different.

🌃 MOJJO Rooftop Lounge Bar
A great spot for city views, drinks and a more intimate rooftop atmosphere.

🌆 SOL & LUNA BKK
Beautiful sunset views, modern design and a really nice dinner setting.

Honestly, Bangkok rooftops just hit different. 😍
