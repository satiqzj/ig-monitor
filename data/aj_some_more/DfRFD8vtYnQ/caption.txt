🚕🇹🇭 YOUR BANGKOK TAXI FARE IS HIGHER THAN THE METER? HERE’S WHY!

Before you think you’re being overcharged in Bangkok, here’s something you should know!

💰 Taxi meter: Your regular metered fare.

✈️ Airport surcharge: An additional ฿50 for taxis using the official airport queue at Suvarnabhumi or Don Mueang.

🛣️ Toll fees: Charged separately when using toll roads.

Not every extra charge is a scam, but knowing the difference can help you avoid paying more than you should.
