5 FREE TEMPLES IN BANGKOK 🇹🇭🚇

Save this list for your next Bangkok trip! Here’s how to reach each temple by MRT, plus one tip for every stop.

1️⃣ Wat Mangkon Kamalawat
🚇 MRT Wat Mangkon
➡️ Walk from the station to the temple in Chinatown.
⭐ Go in the morning to see locals lighting incense and making offerings.

2️⃣ Wat Ratchabophit
🚇 MRT Sam Yot or Sanam Chai
➡️ Take a short tuk tuk or taxi ride from the station.
⭐ Look for the circular courtyard. Around 4 PM is a good time to catch the golden light.

3️⃣ Wat Thepthidaram
🚇 MRT Sam Yot
➡️ Walk through the Old Town to the temple.
⭐ This is a quieter stop if you want to take photos away from the crowds.

4️⃣ Wat Bowonniwet Vihara
🚇 MRT Sam Yot or Sanam Chai
➡️ Head toward the Khao San Road area after leaving the station.
⭐ Pair your temple visit with an evening walk along Khao San Road.

5️⃣ Wat Hong Rattanaram
🚇 MRT Itsaraphap
➡️ Walk along Itsaraphap Road toward the temple.
⭐ Take a closer look at the murals that depict Buddhist Jataka tales.

🙏 All five temples are free to enter. Cover your shoulders and knees, wear comfy shoes, and start early to beat the heat.
