Wat Paknam Pasicharoen
Built: Around 1610, during the Ayutthaya period. The temple has been expanded considerably over the centuries. 

Big Buddha: Phra Buddha Dhammakaya Thep Mongkol, a huge seated bronze Buddha approximately 69 metres high. Construction began in 2017 and was completed/inaugurated in 2021. 

🚇 MRT: Bang Phai Station (Blue Line), Exit 1 — about 10–15 minutes’ walk. This is the easiest rail option. 

🚆 BTS: Wutthakat Station (Silom Line) is a convenient BTS option; alternatively, take BTS to Bang Wa, transfer to the MRT, and travel one stop to Bang Phai. 

📍 Nearby: Khlong Bang Luang / Artist’s House area, Talat Phlu, and Wat Khun Chan are useful places to combine with the temple.

🕗 Visitor hours: A reliable visitor window is 08:00–18:00 daily. Some listings show the wider temple grounds open earlier/later, so for the pagoda and main visitor attractions, I recommend planning for 08:00–18:00. Entrance is free.
