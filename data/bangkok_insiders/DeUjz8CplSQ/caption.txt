🏮 WAT TRAIMIT VEGETARIAN FESTIVAL 2026 🏮

📍 Wat Traimit Withayaram Worawihan
Yaowarat (Chinatown), Bangkok, Thailand

📅 9–18 October 2026

✨ FESTIVAL HIGHLIGHTS

🥢 Vegetarian Food Festival
Enjoy a variety of delicious Thai-Chinese vegetarian dishes and traditional festival specialties.

🙏 Pay Respect to Sacred Buddha Images
Visit the temple and receive blessings while paying respect to:

* The Golden Buddha (Luang Pho Thong Kham) — the world-famous solid gold Buddha image
* Luang Pho Soi
* Luang Pho Samrit

🏮 Auspicious Teng Lang Lanterns
Sponsor a traditional Chinese Teng Lang lantern (โคมเต็งลั้ง) personalized with your name to symbolize good fortune, prosperity, and blessings.

🌙 Evening Lantern Illumination
Admire the beautiful glowing lanterns illuminating the temple grounds after sunset.
