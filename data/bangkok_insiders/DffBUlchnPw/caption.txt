LV visionary journeys | A Journey Through Fashion, Art & Luxury
Discover the world of Louis Vuitton through an immersive exhibition celebrating the brand’s heritage, craftsmanship, creativity and spirit of travel.
Visitor Information — November 2026
Location: Gaysorn Amarin, Ploenchit Road, Bangkok
Nearest BTS: Chit Lom (approximately 3–5 minutes’ walk)
Opening hours: Daily, 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
Admission: FREE
Reservation: Advance online booking recommended
Suggested visit: 30–60 minutes
Exhibition Highlights
Trunkscape: Spectacular installation featuring 96 Louis Vuitton Monogram trunks.
Origins: Discover the history of Louis Vuitton, founded in Paris in 1854.
Asnières: Explore the historic family home and workshop.
Workshop: Experience the artistry behind luxury trunk-making.
Fashion & Collaborations: Discover iconic designs and artistic partnerships.
Souvenirs: A playful finale with collectible keepsakes.
