LSA Events Recap: Here’s our event recap from 1-31 July 2026 

1. Gaa - Thonglor 
Chef Garima Arora introduces a new menu and a new adventure through the Indian state of Bihar. 

 2. Bianca launches catering service - Bianca Terminal 21 Asoke 
Italian restaurant Bianca showcases their catering services for events 

3. Chai Jia Chai x La Bourriche 133 x Odette 
A special collaboration dinner between friends and stellar chefs, Chai Jia Chai welcomed La Bourriche 133 and Odette. 

4. Jiwan - Siam Kempinski Hotel Bangkok 
Alain Ducasse’s Doha restaurant Jiwan popped up at ALATi for two delicious weeks this July. 

5. Rangoon Tea House x Jhol - Thonglor 
Rangoon Tea House and Jhol collaborated for one fantastic “Biryani Brunch.” 
 
6. Tax Cocktail Bar - Chinatown 
Tax Cocktail Bar launches their Insect Cocktail Menu, featuring real insects as bar snack. 

7. The Art of Kimoto - Akira Back 
Akira Back hosted Japan’s historic Kurosawa Brewery with an exclusive six-course Maruto Sake dinner. 

8. Hideaki Sato x Dej Kewkacha - Mooncake launch 
Chef Hideaki Sato of 3-Michelin-star fame and award-winning chef Dej Kewkacha team up to launch mooncakes. 

9. Honeybee Seoul Afternoon Tea - The Ritz-Carlton Bangkok 
The Ritz-Carlton presents the adorable Honeybee Seoul Afternoon Tea at Caleo.
