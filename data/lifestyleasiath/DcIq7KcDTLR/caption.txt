Plastic packaging is so commonplace nowadays that we don’t second-guess seeing it.

It’s great to see a Thai supermarket taking climate change seriously by reducing plastic waste when wrapping fresh produce. This initiative is featured in the Jing Jai Farmers' Market at Tops supermarkets nationwide.

This isn't just helping local farmers in Thailand, but also the environment by wrapping the produce found in this special section with nature-based packaging. As a shopper, it's a delight to discover the different kinds of packaging being used and know it's helping reduce plastic waste.
 
If you want to see it for yourself, head over to your local Tops supermarket to do a little grocery shopping, and take joy in knowing you helped make the Earth a little greener.
