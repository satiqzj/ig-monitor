Bruno Mars announces his return to Thailand with two shows in Bangkok on 24 and 25 April 2027. The concerts are part of ‘The Romantic Tour,’ which will also see stops in Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan, South Korea, and the Philippines.

Mars has also teased a special performance by Silk Sonic, and performances by Raye and Tyla in select cities. The general ticket sale opens on 14 September on LiveNation Tero. Tap link in bio for details.
