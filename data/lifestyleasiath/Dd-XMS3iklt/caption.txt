Choo-Choo: the Kingdom is getting a new way to travel slowly. In 2027, Eastern & Oriental Express is expected to return with its first Thailand-focused journey, developed by Belmond and TAT.
 
The proposed route will connect Bangkok, Kanchanaburi, Hua Hin, Hat Yai, and Padang Besar, combining scenic rail travel with fine dining, Thai culture, local crafts, and lesser-visited destinations. The itinerary and onboard experience are still in development under TAT’s “Healing is the New Luxury” initiative.
