The Bangkok International Film Festival (BKKIF) made its comeback last year after a 17-year hiatus, and now it returns for a brand new edition in 2026. Running from 13–27 September, the Festival boasts a stellar lineup of films this year, composed of movies from India, Germany, Japan, Greece, Thailand, and many more.
