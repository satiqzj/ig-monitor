Negroni Week is back for its 14th edition, brought to Thailand by Campari and Alchemy Wines and Spirits. Running globally from 21–27 September, this year‘s celebration kicked off at Stella, Capella Bangkok with an exclusive opening night featuring special guests Monica Berg and Alex Kratena, globally renowned bartenders. From there, the week unfolds across the city with a lineup of world-renowned bartenders headlining guest shifts and events for all cocktail lovers to enjoy.
