To kick off Negroni Week 2026 with Campari, the Four Seasons Hotel Bangkok is pouring its way across Asia with a special selection of cocktails. Bringing together signature serves from Four Seasons bars in Hangzhou, Koh Samui, Shenzhen, Jakarta, Singapore, and Seoul, guests can enjoy a taste of unique Negronis from across the region. They’ll be available in Bangkok throughout Negroni Week, from 21-27 September.
