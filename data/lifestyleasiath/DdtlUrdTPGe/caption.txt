Watch collectors and enthusiasts alike were treated to a special symposium at Bangkok Watch Week this evening. Hosted by Siam Paragon, speaker Cole Pennington took guests on a journey through 100 years of Tudor, going through the archives to explore the brand’s rich history and the remarkable figures who have helped make its watches so beloved. Pennington also highlighted Southeast Asia, and Thailand in particular, as one of the world’s most exciting markets for watch lovers. We couldn’t agree more.
