In a special awards ceremony at the Sindhorn Kempinski tonight, @bkkbarshow crowned the 20 best bars in Thailand, as voted for by local bar industry insiders.

Beyond the ranking, special awards also went to Mod Kaew Wine Bar @modkaewbkk (Wine Bar of the Year), The Norm @thenormbangkok (Rooftop Bar of the Year), BKK Social Club @bkksocialclub (Art of Hospitality), Dry Wave Cocktail Studio @drywavecocktailstudio (Bar Team of the Year), Tax Bar @tax_bar_bkk (Menu Design of the Year), Tamaryn Cooper @tamaryn_cooper (Emerging Bartender of the Year), and Palm Supawit @supawit_palm (Bartender of the Year). 

Congratulations to all the big winners!
