LSA Events Recap: Here’s our event recap from 1 - 30 September 2026 
 
1. Big Bad Wolf Books is back, celebrating the 10th anniversary since its first book festival here in Bangkok 
 
2. A look at Jim Thompson’s city-focused capsule collection: ‘The Urban Edit.’ 
 
3. Longines unveiled the new chapter of The Longines Master Collection together with friends of Longines at The Norm Bangkok. 

4. The California coffee house has finally arrived in the Big Mango 
 
5. DIE HARD’s pastrami sandwiches are the talk of the talk of town, with long lines everyday 
 
6. Chef Massimiliano Prete and Chef Andrea Accordi teamed up for a delicious special pizza menu 

7. Anne-Sophie Pic at Le Normandie celebrated its first anniversary with a special tasting menu in September. 

8. The first Fairmont in Thailand has opened on Sukhumvit 20, with interiors inspired by Thai floral heritage. 

9. Bringing something fresh to the bar-hopping neighbourhood, Rosa opens as a “crossroads cuisine” restaurant in Chinatown’s Soi Nana. 

10. Anantara Siam celebrates the end of its annual World Gourmet Festival with a special Sunday brunch. 

11. IGNIV welcomed Andreas Caminada for a brief visit, where he officially introduced Dominik Falk, IGNIV Bangkok’s new Head Chef.
