A signature motif gets a colourful new life in Bangkok. Van Cleef & Arpels (@vancleefarpels) brings the Perlée universe to Préf Café & Bar, with French artist Arthur Hoffner reimagining the Maison’s golden beads through playful sculptures, bold shapes and vibrant colours. Alongside the art, visitors can explore Perlée jewellery and timepieces, as well as special pieces from the Maison’s heritage. Open from 1 to 18 October at Préf Café & Bar, Soi Sathorn 11, the pop-up offers a closer look at the creativity and craftsmanship behind the collection.
