Among the 11 venues hosting this year’s Bangkok Art Biennale, BACC is one of the main exhibition spaces, showcasing works by 36 artists. Themed ‘Angels & Mara,’ the artworks explore the contradictions of contemporary life. It’s free to visit and runs until 28 February 2027.
