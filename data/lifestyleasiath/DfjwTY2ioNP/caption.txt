For the past ten years, Cocotte has built a reputation as a place to gather for residents of Phrom Phong and beyond. Birthdays, after-work wind-downs, family get-togethers—you name it, Cocotte was an unmistakable go-to. After being renovated, Cocotte reopened in July 2026, returning with a fresh and bold look while still keeping in mind what made it a crowd-favourite: that at the end of it all, it’s a place where people get together.
