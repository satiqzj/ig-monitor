Bangkok’s river is about to host one of Thailand’s rarest spectacles.

On November 6, 52 royal barges and 2,200 oarsmen will form a procession stretching around 1.2km down the Chao Phraya for the Royal Kathin ceremony marking Queen Suthida’s 48th birthday. The tradition stretches back centuries and is rarely seen in modern Bangkok.

📅 November 6, 2026, from 2.30pm

🚣 Rehearsals: August 17, 20, 27 / September 3, 8, 17, 23 / October 2, 8, 15

🎽 Full-dress rehearsals: October 21 and 28 – the closest preview of the real event

📍 Wasukri Pier to Wat Arun Ratchawararam, along the Chao Phraya

👀 Best viewing: along either riverbank between Krung Thon Bridge and Wat Arun, especially around Wat Arun and the Grand Palace side. Arrive early – river traffic is restricted during rehearsals and viewing spots fill fast
