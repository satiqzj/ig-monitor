Fancy coming face-to-face with a T-rex without leaving the city? 🦖 Down at Asiatique The Riverfront Destination, on the banks of the Chao Phraya, Jurassic World: The Experience Bangkok turns one this month, so to mark the occasion the blockbuster attraction rolls out a whole programme of extras.

Wander through and you meet life-size giants up close, the kind of buzz that pulls crowds from all over the globe. Expect open-air screenings that carry you back through every chapter of the saga, all playing out against the water after dark. Then comes the big one, a dazzling fireworks display at 8.09pm on August 8.

Better yet, from August 8-30 anyone flashing a ticket or E-ticket bags free run of the VIP Zone, no extra charge 🦕
