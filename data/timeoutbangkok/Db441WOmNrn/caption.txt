Things got a little steamy for our editor at Bisou 💋

It started innocently enough. A clarified Bloody Mary, a little French toast, some wine. Then the menu started flirting and, before he knew it, a climax of beef-filled puff pastry sealed the night.

@bisoubangkok describes itself as ‘simple and sexy’, and Bangkok’s seriously unserious modern French restaurant certainly knows how to seduce. Consider this our editor’s big, sloppy French kiss to one of Bangkok’s most tantalising fine-dining restaurants.
