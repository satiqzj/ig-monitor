Remember DOMi & JD BECK? If you were one of the lucky few crammed inside their sold-out Bangkok debut nearly two years ago, you already know why the city's buzzing again. And if you missed it, here's your second chance 🎹

The two-time GRAMMY-nominated duo, French keyboardist DOMi Louna and Texan drummer JD Beck, return on November 24, taking over Sphere Hall at EMSPHERE for a show that bends every rule you thought jazz had.

They arrive with a brand new record too. 'WHO ASKED?' drops via Anderson .Paak's APESHIT imprint alongside the legendary Blue Note, spiritual home to the genre's greats. Expect fresh material and the same gloriously unpredictable spark, so the only way to find out is to be in the room.
