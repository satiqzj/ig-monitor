Remember Nene Royal? The 16-year-old shredder is back on America's Got Talent – and once again floors the room, this time with a blistering cover of a Muse classic 🎸

This season’s new ’Judges’ Callbacks’ round gives hopefuls one last shot at the live shows, and Royal grabs hers with both hands. Her first audition,  a two-hand tapping take on the Cranberries’ ‘Zombie’, earned four yeses. Now she tackles ’Hysteria’ – 'and makes it look effortless.

She opens with a lightning-fast lead break, powers through Matt Bellamy’s soaring melody, weaves the growling bassline underneath and belts out the vocals over the top. Two guitar parts at once? She barely blinks – extraordinary work from someone who wasn’t even born when the song was released.

AGT drops the clip early on August 8 with the cheeky title ‘Nene Royal CRUSHES Hysteria by Muse’, ahead of the full episode’s August 11 broadcast. It races past 2.1 million views within 24 hours – and frankly,we’re not surprised 🤯
