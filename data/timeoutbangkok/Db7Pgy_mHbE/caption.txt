Historical dramas, mind-bending sci-fi and big-hearted musicals 🇺🇸 – that's the line-up the US Embassy has ready to mark 250 years since America signed its Declaration of Independence.
'Freedom 250: American Film Retrospective' lands at the AUA Auditorium in Bangkok on August 29-30, and cinema fans, this one's a proper treat. A weekend, the silver screen and decades of classics that shaped a nation. 

Every screening runs in English with Thai subtitles, and best of all, it's completely free. Check out the full programme and mark your calendar 🎟️🍿
