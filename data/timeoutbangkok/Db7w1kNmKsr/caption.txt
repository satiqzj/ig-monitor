Roll up, roll up. The big top pitches on the riverside this September, and it comes packed with RuPaul royalty.

@drthaifans, the crew behind some of Bangkok's most talked-about international showcases, puts on its flagship event, Drag Race Thai Fans vs The World 3, at the Calypso Bangkok Theatre, Asiatique The Riverfront, on September 6. A circus-inspired theme sets the tone for an evening of spectacle, glamour and serious star power.

Three of the artform's brightest names lead the bill. Raja Gemini, winner of RuPaul's Drag Race Season 3, makes her Thai stage debut.  Alongside her are Morphine Love Dion, the razor-witted Season 16 finalist, and Gawdland, the Chiang Mai-born talent who this year became the first from Thailand to win UK vs the World. 
One beloved tradition returns too. The Best Drag Look contest opens to every queen in the room, so bring your A-game ✨

Tickets are on sale now, grab yours at @drthaifans’s link in bio.
