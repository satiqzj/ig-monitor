Remember belting out 'I'm just like you. You're just like me' as a kid? On September 20, that same wave of nostalgia sweeps through Bangkok as a full symphony orchestra brings the beloved scores from the animated Barbie films to glorious life. 🎀

A line-up of gifted singers joins the players onstage to perform those childhood favourites, promising one gorgeously dreamy afternoon or evening. 

Catch it at Siampic Hall on the 7th floor of Siam Square One, with two shows to pick from at 2pm and 7pm. Tickets run from B1,600 to B4,000 through @etcstudio.bkk’ s link in bio 🩰🦢
