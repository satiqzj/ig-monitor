‘The Tide & The Tower’: a Michelin-starred meeting of two culinary worlds this weekend only at lebua! 

For two nights only, lebua brings together two Michelin-starred chefs for a rare collaboration between Chef Matthew Kammerer of America’s Harbor House Inn and Chef Ryuki Kawasaki of lebua’s Mezzaluna – uniting their distinct culinary perspectives and blending sustainable, ingredient-driven cooking with Japanese precision and French technique. 

Together, they create a six-course dinner built around a shared passion for exceptional ingredients for just one weekend. 

Taking place at Mezzaluna by lebua on July 24-25, this exclusive collaboration is limited, but oh so worth it…
