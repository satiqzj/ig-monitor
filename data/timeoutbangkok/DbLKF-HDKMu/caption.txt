This Bangkok story begins where fashion meets faith: with someone distracted by a Buddha stall outside a temple. This time, the distraction became a brand.

This is @khongbangkok’s story: Buddhas, crosses, Ganesha and Thai spirits, reimagined as jewellery to reach for every day – or never take off – without losing what makes them sacred. We sat down with founder Sophie Schuemers-Rogers to talk faith, fashion and the one piece she is still waiting for.
