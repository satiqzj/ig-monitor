Why is everyone talking about this organic, eight-seat omakase? Because sometimes, the best restaurants let the food speak for itself. 

Hidden away in Langsuan is Sushi Kuuya (@sushikuuyabkk), an intimate eight-seat restaurant led by Chef Goji Kobayashi where the ingredients do all the talking. With more than 18 years of experience across Japan, the US and Thailand, his omakase is rooted in traditional sushi techniques, showcasing seasonal produce and quality seasonal seafood flown in from his hometown of Fukuoka.

The name itself tells you everything you need to know. Kuuya comes from a Hakata dialect meaning ‘let's eat’ – a fitting nod to Chef Goji's family roots, and an invitation that's hard to refuse.

Forget flashy presentations or unnecessary theatrics. Every course is guided by a philosophy of simplicity, precision and respect, allowing every ingredient to shine. With just eight seats and two services each evening, dinner feels more like a conversation between chef and guest than your typical fast-paced omakase experience.

If your idea of a memorable omakase is exceptional seafood, meticulous craftsmanship and an experience that's as personal as it is delicious, this Langsuan gem deserves a place on your list.

So, what are you waiting for? Let’s eat 🍣👇
