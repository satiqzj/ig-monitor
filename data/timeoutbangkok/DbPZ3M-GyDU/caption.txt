Siam Carnival Funfair is back July 31-August 16 🎡🎠🎢

Returning from its seven-year hiatus, there’s thrilling rides, classic carnival games and plenty of food and snacks to be found. 

As the biggest mobile amusement park in Thailand, the fair pulls out all the stops with the Ferris wheel to roller coasters imported from Germany and Italy. 

Just remember the golden rule: ride first, eat later.
