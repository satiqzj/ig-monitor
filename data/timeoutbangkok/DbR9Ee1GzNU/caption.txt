Missing Japan? Nippon Haku brings the best of Japan to Bangkok from August 28-30.

Described as ‘one of Thailand’s largest Japan-focused expos,’ the event returns to the city with all the favourites from authentic Japanese food, cultural activities, J-pop performances and lifestyle products you wanted but couldn’t fit in your suitcase on your last trip. 

Planning a trip to Japan? Then check out the travel desk or find something more permanent at the study abroad and career opportunities area and really lock in the dream.
