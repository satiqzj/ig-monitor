What does ‘womb’ mean to you? 🤰

At first glance, Womb looks like an exhibition about something all women have in common. Spend a little longer with it, though, and it becomes something far more personal.

Created by artist Gerda Liebmann, the exhibition brings together 24 women, 24 portraits and 24 written reflections that reveal how differently each person relates to their womb. For some, it's tied to motherhood. For others, grief, identity, independence, pain or personal choice. Together, they challenge the assumption that a shared body part means a shared experience.

Running until August 14 at BATURU Incubator Space, just a short walk from MRT Sam Yan, our guest writer explores this thoughtful exhibition that asks more questions than it answers.
