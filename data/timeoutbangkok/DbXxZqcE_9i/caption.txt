Every Friday at 8.30pm, while much of Bangkok heads to the bars, @bangkokherping explores a very different kind of nightlife.

Four-metre pythons in the canals. Vipers by the dozen once the city’s parks go dark. Vine snakes living metres from a skewer stall for 15 years, completely unnoticed – until now.

We spoke to the team behind Bangkok’s most unusual nights out about fear, fireflies and what is really living alongside us after dark.
