PREP, the London indie-pop band that Thai fans have taken firmly to heart, come back to Bangkok on October 17 for Live in Bangkok 2026 at Melt Livehouse, Cloud 11 Hall. It sits under the Maho Rasop Series from Seen Scene Space, so the room fills fast 🎶

They form in 2015, a gang of studio tinkerers raised on glossy '80s sheen, slow-simmer R&B and loose jazz, then fold the lot together until it sounds like no one else going. Music for a long taxi ride home, the kind you catch yourself humming.

This round they air cuts from new EP 'One Day In The Sun', so a handful of unfamiliar ones brush up against the singalongs.

Tickets run B3,500 for Zone A and B2,500 for Zone B. Pre-sale opens July 29 at 10am-July 30, 11:59pm, general sale from July 31 at 10am.
