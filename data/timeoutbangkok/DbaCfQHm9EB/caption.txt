Tokyo’s ’80s City Pop summer returns to Bangkok with Hiroshi Nagai’s Summer Dreaming exhibition at River City 🏝️⛱️🌞🌊

For his first-ever solo exhibition in Thailand, get up close with more than 30 rare original works, alongside 4 new paintings created exclusively for this show. Don’t miss this nostalgic journey from August 4-September 4 with free admission.
