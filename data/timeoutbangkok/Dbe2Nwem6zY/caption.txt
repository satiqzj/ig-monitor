The San Francisco-born singer who first found fame with Girls' Generation brings 'Tiffany Young: Edge of Calm Tour in Bangkok' to town, a night toasting ten years of going it alone. It comes on Sunday, October 25 at @uoblive_bangkok, sixth floor of EmSphere, the big arena above the shops that you reach by escalator past the food halls. Ten years solo, one Bangkok stage.

Tiffany Young’s Edge of Calm Asia Tour dates:

September 12 – Hong Kong
September 19 – Jakarta
September 26 – Singapore
October 9 – Taipei
October 10 – Taipei
October 17 – Ho Chi Minh City
October 25 – Bangkok
December 27 – Seoul

Tickets open on Friday, August 28. UOB cardholders grab first from 12pm to 2pm, then general sale follows at 6pm. Prices run B6,600, B5,500, B4,600, B3,500 and B2,900, so most budgets get a look-in 🎟️
