August 12 is coming, marking Wan Mae, Thailand's Mother's Day, held every year on the birthday of the late Her Majesty Queen Sirikit, revered as the Mother of the Nation.

The word แม่ (mae) does a lot in one syllable: affection, authority and a promise that whatever's coming is made properly, from a recipe nobody's allowed to touch. You'll spot it on shopfronts, sauce bottles and restaurant signs, shorthand for 'don't ask, just eat'.
