This August, Bangkok Youth Opera hands three heavyweight works to a cast of young people and dares them to pull it off 🎭

First, Gluck's Orfeo ed Euridice – the old myth of a musician chasing his love down to the land of the dead. Darren Royston moves Orpheus across time and dimensions, holograms glitching over the walls, that fatal rule still in place: no glancing back. You know how well that goes.

Then Don Juan, the 1762 reform ballet, sets undying loyalty against a bottomless appetite. Clever programming, one feeling running two ways.

Finally Don Pasquale lands its Thailand premiere, relocated from 19th-century Rome to present-day Bangkok, all AI worries and social-media pressure. Expect big laughs and a plot that stings.

All at the Studio, 4th Floor, Bangkok Art and Culture Centre. Tickets are B1,200 general, B600 for students with ID.
