Remember 'blue'? The one you play on loop until the neighbours know the words, the gentle guitar that carries you through slow, do-nothing afternoons? 🎧 

Good news, then. yung kai comes back to Thailand for 'yung kai – stay with the ocean, i'll find you: Asia 2026' at Sphere Hall, EMSHERE on November 1.

The Chinese-Canadian indie pop darling arrives fresh off his 2025 debut album, which shares its name with the tour. It's all warm, easy-listening melodies stitched with the kind of sincerity that makes you want to text someone you mis.

Presale runs August 13-15, and general sale opens August 16 at 10am. Set a reminder, yeah?
