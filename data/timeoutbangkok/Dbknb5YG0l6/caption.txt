This weekend, August 7-9, @skylinefilmbkk teams up with @shanghai_mansion to turn the hotel’s 1930s Shanghai-style setting into an open-air cinema in the heart of Yaowarat. 

Three Wong Kar-wai classics anchor the six-film line-up. In the Mood for Love is already sold out, but Chungking Express and Fallen Angels remain, alongside Comrades: Almost a Love Story, Pulp Fiction and Pretty Woman.

Tickets are B450, including a drink, with screenings from 5.30pm.

Check @skylinefilmbkk for booking details.
