The magic returns, and this year it comes with a 92-piece orchestra breathing down your neck 🪄 

Grab your broom, because the Triwizard Tournament touches down in Bangkok. 'Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire in Concert' takes over @uoblive_bangkok at Emsphere on Saturday November 21 at 7pm.

The full film plays across a 40-foot screen in crisp high-definition while Patrick Doyle's score gets performed live, every last note, by orchestra and choir under the baton of Timothy Henty. Dragons, mermaids, a maze that wants you good and lost – all of it sounding out in the room. 

Tickets open on August 8 at noon ⚡
