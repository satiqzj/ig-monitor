DIAGE Festival is back, landing at its new home, Cloud 11 on Sukhumvit 101, from October 31-November1.

First staged in 2023, the digital art and electronic music festival pairs immersive visuals with sets from Thai and international DJs, electronic acts and live bands. Daytime live coding sessions, talks and workshops add even more to explore.

The comeback also brings ‘Continental Affair (DIAGE Deconstruction)’, a reimagining of the cult track by pioneering Thai electronic musician Cyndi Seui, aka Cesar B. de Guzman – a fitting nod to the local scene’s roots.

Blind Tickets go on sale soon, before the line-up is announced, with limited quantities available. Register at diagefestival.com for first access.
