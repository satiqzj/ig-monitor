Everyone's invited to wind the clock back to the 1930s at 'Swing in the Park: SWING THROUGH TIME', a dance night that brings the legendary Lumpini Hall back to life 🎷

The ballroom sits right by the park's edge, and at more than 90 years old it's one of the oldest surviving dance venues in the city. For one evening it fills again with music, live performances and plenty of vintage footwork.

The Bangkok Metropolitan Administration teams up with Bangkok Swing to flood the floor with warm lighting, big sound and live sets from The Stumblrs and Suntharaporn. Never swung a step in your life? Doesn't matter, babe – a beginner-friendly open class sorts you out before you loop arms with strangers and dance the night down.
It's free. Saturday August 15, 5-8pm. Frock up and go ✨
