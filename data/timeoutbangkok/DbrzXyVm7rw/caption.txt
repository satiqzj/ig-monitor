The King of Rock and Roll is back on the big screen. @thaifilmarchive is marking the anniversary of Elvis Presley’s death with two free films at historic Nang Loeng Cinema.

 🎬 Aug 15: Elvis (2022), the biopic nominated for eight Oscars
 🎬 Aug 16: Blue Hawaii (1961), featuring Can't Help Falling in Love’

Both films start at 4pm and entry is free. Tickets are available at the door from 3pm on a first-come, first-served basis, with no online booking. There are only 250 seats and no parking, so get there early and take public transport.
