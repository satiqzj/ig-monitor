Clear your weekend and give your favourite reads the send-off they deserve at the Reshelf Secondhand Book Market – the first-ever market from @helloreshelf, the platform where secondhand books change hands and start again.

The market runs August 29-30, 10am-6pm, at @ninetails.on.radio, and it costs you nothing.

Stalls come stacked with pre-loved paperbacks and hardbacks, priced to move. Then comes the Blind Book Swap, you wrap a read you're ready to release, decorate it and pass it to a stranger. Browse, collect stickers, redeem a Lucky Draw, or write a note to your future self and it will reach you at the end of the year, straight from you to you. 

One thing before you go: bring a tote bag!
