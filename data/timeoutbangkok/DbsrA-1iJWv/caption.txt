Not an easy crowd to impress, but Bangkok's got Gen Z's vote 🏆

This year, to curate the list with Intrepid Travel, Time Out narrowed its 2026 survey down to under-30s only. It makes sense, and  filtered that hard, we land at #10 globally – still Thailand's entry is the only Southeast Asian city on the list.

96 percent of young Bangkokians say the city makes them happy – happier than anywhere else they've lived. 87 percent rated the restaurant scene highly. 78 percent said the same for nightlife.

Congrats, Bangkok. And to everyone making it feel this alive – you know who you are 🩷
