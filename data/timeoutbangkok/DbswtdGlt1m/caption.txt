Bangkok’s food scene is in playful form this week. KAYAKI (@kayakithailand) opens its third branch at The EmQuartier with a Toyosu-inspired seafood set; Pinki’s Deli (@pinkisdeli.bkk) turns plara bong and other cult flavours into spreads; Yu Ting Yuan (@yutingyuanbkk) welcomes dim sum chef Zhao Yan; and Arva (@arvabangkok) launches a leisurely Italian-style weekday lunch at Aman Nai Lert Bangkok (@amannailertbangkok). Mother’s Day brings a guest-chef brunch at Lily’s (@lilysbkk) and coconut-themed afternoon tea at Caleō (@caleobkk), both at The Ritz Carlton, Bangkok (@theritzcarltonbangkok).
