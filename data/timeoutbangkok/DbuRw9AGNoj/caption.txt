August in Bangkok means rain and lots of it. But rather than sulk under an umbrella or scroll your phone in a mall food court, why not let an orchestra soundtrack your month instead? 
Lumphini Hall throws open its doors for a run of free performances. Eight events across the month, most kicking off around 5pm, so you can wander over after work, guitar solos backed by a full orchestra, a classical series and ballroom nights.
