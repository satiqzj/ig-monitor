Thailand celebrates its own Mother’s Day, Wan Mae, on August 12, so the evening before GO GRRRLS crowns a rather different matriarch with CLUB CONFESSIONS 👑💅

FV39 on Sukhumvit 39 hosts proceedings from 9pm ‘til 2am on August 11, split across two rooms with wildly contrasting agendas. The Confessions Lounge is the soppy one, all heartfelt sing-alongs and forgotten deep cuts. Next door, Confessions on the Dancefloor asks nothing but movement, sweat and release. 

Queen JAI SIRA headlines an exclusive Confessions II set, while Azxula and crew bring vogue and ballroom to the floor. A rotating cast of DJs keeps things pumping, among them Dookie, Peachji, Soshine, Craskey, Khun Na and Happy Bara, with the one and only Chaofah Van Cartier presiding as door diva and host.

Rock up in full drag and you sail past the queue for free. Tix starts at B450 for an early-bird spot online, B550 in advance or B650 on the night, a complimentary drink included. Grab your at @gogrrrls’s link in bio
