Here's some in-flight trivia for you… meet the yaks.

Those giant statues watching you at Suvarnabhumi? Each one's identifiable by face colour, weapon and posture – and each comes with its own rank, backstory and, often, a grudge!

Each of the 12 statues lining the fourth-floor departure hall is a named character from the Ramakien, Thailand's version of the Indian Ramayana – replicas of the figures found at Wat Phra Kaew, the Temple of the Emerald Buddha.

In Thai belief, giants like these guard temple gates to keep evil spirits out. Airports of Thailand put them here for the same reason: to watch over travellers as they come and go.
