August in Thailand is all about honouring mothers and honestly, what says ‘I love you, Mum’ better than a day out surrounded by flowers?

For eight days only, Queen Sirikit Park turns technicolour, home to more than 260,000 flowering plants. Celosia, globe amaranth, impatiens, American marigolds, cosmos, zinnias and orchids sit in a riot of shades that practically begs for the camera. 

Take a slow lap of the grounds, because fresh corners keep appearing every few steps. Our top tip? Rock up late afternoon, when the golden light hits just right and the whole place glows. The sweetener? You walk away with a free tree seedling for the garden.

The Colourful Flora Festival runs from August 8-13, 10am-8pm, at Queen Sirikit Park in Chatuchak.
