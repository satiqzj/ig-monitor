Thailand’s Oscar pick is here – and most of us haven’t seen it yet.

9 Temples to Heaven, Sompot Chidgasornpongse’s first narrative feature, has been selected as Thailand’s submission for Best International Feature Film at the 99th Academy Awards.

The family drama opens the Bangkok International Film Festival on September 13 before reaching cinemas nationwide on September 17.
