Daft Punk split in 2021 and rarely toured before that, so for most of us a good tribute is as close as it gets, and ‘Daft Funk Live’ are a proper one. The two brothers behind the act started it in 2018 and take it seriously, with a full band on live drums, bass and guitar, plus those replica robot suits, glowing LED visors and all. Now, they bring the full show to Mustache Bangkok on Saturday January 16 2027.

Songs run from Homework to Random Access Memories, so expect 'One More Time', 'Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger' and a few reworked mixes, all louder than the records and nearer than any arena. 

Bangkok rounds off Midnight Sun's three-city Thai tour, following Koh Phangan on January 13 and Phuket on January 14.

Tickets are B990 and go on sale on September 29.
