Calling all cat parents 🐾

Ma Ma Meow brings 1,000+ cat stamps from around the world to Bangkok, alongside 100 reader-submitted cats and their stories. You can also turn a photo of your cat into a stamp design or fold an origami feline for the exhibition.
Keep an eye out for a series of especially cute stamps to track down around the museum, too.

📍@thaistampmuseum, Saphan Kwai (BTS Saphan Kwai)
📅 Until October 18, 2026
⏰ Wed–Sun, 8.30am–4.30pm (closed Mon–Tue and public holidays)
