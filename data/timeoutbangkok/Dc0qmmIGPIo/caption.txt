Most of us are winded after a flight of stairs, so spare a thought for everyone lining up against Sawang Janpram. 

The 107-year-old from Thailand is the oldest of more than 11,000 athletes at this year's World Masters Athletics Championships in Daegu, South Korea, and he flies home with two golds 🥇

He throws the discus 7.59 metres on August 27, the only entrant over 100, then adds javelin gold on August 29. Here's the part that gets us. Sawang only starts exercising at 80, when his daughter Siripan, now 74 and his coach, talks him round. Around 78 medals follow. 'I hope young people remember this and exercise every day,' he says. 

At 107, he still trains, still throws and still wins 💪
