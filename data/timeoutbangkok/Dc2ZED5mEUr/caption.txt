Fancy a run that doesn't cost a baht? Bangkok's city administration has one lined up, and everyone who registers walks away with a commemorative medal.

The free morning takes over People's Plaza at Parliament House on Sunday, October 4, put on by the Faculty of Medicine Vajira Hospital alongside the Dusit and Bang Sue district offices. 

Pick your distance from 3km, 5km or 10km, with routes winding through both neighbourhoods and past Vajira. Registration opens on September 9 at 9am through the Vajira Hospital's website, and you wear whatever you like. A B250 donation gets you the official 'Healthy City' shirt, collected in person only, and counts as a double tax deduction.
