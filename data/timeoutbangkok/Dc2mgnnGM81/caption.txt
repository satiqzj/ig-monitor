September is here, putting the year-end festivities within sight. Until then, Bangkok’s food scene has plenty to keep us occupied.
This week brings mooncakes, wagyu, wok hei and a Sunday roast fuelled by Champagne. Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok (@kimptonmaalaibangkok) unveils a Fire Horse mooncake box, Capricci (@capricci_bkk) gives wagyu the Italian treatment and Seasonal Tastes (@westinbangkok) explores Cantonese and Shandong cooking. In Tha Tien, Mitr Street (@mitrstreet.bkk) fills a six-storey shophouse with Yaowarat-style Thai-Chinese food and Wat Pho views. The Standard Grill (@thestandardgrillbangkok) upgrades its Weekend Roast with free-flow Veuve Clicquot, while the new Surawong izakaya Kizaru (@kizaru_bangkok) channels late-night Tokyo with sharing plates and regional sake.
