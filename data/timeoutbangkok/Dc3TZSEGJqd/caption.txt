Dreaming of a long, lazy Thailand trip? Check the small print before you book, because the entry rules read very differently now.

Four announcements in the country's Royal Gazette, all signed by Anutin Charnvirakul, who signs off as both prime minister and interior minister, put 93 countries in three tiers. Most visitors drop from a 60-day stay to 30, and land-border crossings now cap at twice a year, so the classic visa-run shuffle over to Laos has a firm limit.

The real catch sits in the wording. That 60-day pass once stretched across tourism, quick business trips and urgent work. The fresh 30-day and 15-day versions say 'for tourism' and nothing else, so rock up for meetings and you need a visa that matches the job.

Each rule starts 15 days after it appears, and anyone who lands before then keeps the stay they get.
