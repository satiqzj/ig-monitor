September in Bangkok means rain, and plenty of it. Rather than sulk under an umbrella all month, though, let a live orchestra handle the soundtrack. Lumphini Hall opens its doors for a run of free weekend concerts, staged in the park while the wet season hammers away outside.

The bill spreads across three afternoons and moves from big band jazz to classical arrangements and traditional Thai music. 

Catch the event at Lumphini Hall, Lumphini Park, from September 12-20. Entry is free.
