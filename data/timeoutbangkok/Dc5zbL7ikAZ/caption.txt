The BKK Vinyl Fair is back, this time at REM Hotel. We didn’t miss the chance to catch up with some cool people and familiar faces from the local scene, as well as the shop owners running stalls, having a nosy at the records in their hands to see what everyone’s after. Mostly rare pressings and the odd lucky find.

If you wanna go dig yourself, now’s your chance. The fair is on from today ‘til Sunday Sep 6, 1pm-10pm, with more than 30 selected shops take part, alongside a Listening Library, workshops, DJ sets and talk sessions. The one to catch is the Live Recording Session, where you watch performances cut straight to wax in real time. You can also pre-order a copy to take home with you.

Tickets are B300 and can be purchased at the venue, and your ticket swaps for a B100 coupon off any record you buy on the day.

What would you wanna find there? Watch on!
