A tree near your home sounds like an ordinary thing to have. But the temperature keeps climbing and the city keeps filling up with buildings, roads and concrete, so a patch of greenery matters more to the rest of us than it ever did.

The city has a plan and it goes by ‘Urban Forest’ – more than just carving out a handful of new parks. Bangkok wants to plant fresh saplings, look after the giants already standing and stitch its scattered greenery together as one connected network.

Keep an eye on the hunt for ‘heritage trees’, too. These grand old survivors matter for wildlife, history and the character of a street. Locals get a say as well, from planting and watering to flagging the ones worth saving nearby.

A greener Bangkok might start with a single tree 🌳
