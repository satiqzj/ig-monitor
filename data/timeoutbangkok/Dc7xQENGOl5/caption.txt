If you've ever peeled yourself off a vinyl seat on one of Bangkok's ‘hot buses’, the BMTA finally has a fix. Enter 1,520 electric replacements, all air-conditioned and fitted with CCTV, GPS and step-free access. 

The first 500 arrive in March 2027 and the rest by May, running on nearly 50 of the city's 107 or so routes. Fares climb to between B15 and B25 by distance, up from B8, as the agency swaps owning and maintaining the fleet for leasing it from private operators. For anyone sick of sweating through the ride home, Bangkok looks a lot more bearable.
