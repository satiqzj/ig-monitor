What do you think about new blue licence plates? 

Thailand’s DLT is considering introducing blue plates for fully electric vehicles, making them easier to identify – particularly in emergencies.
Nothing is confirmed just yet, though, and the DLT wants to hear what the public thinks before moving ahead.
