Say the words 'Lakhon Nok' and most of us picture a stiff, traditional Thai performance lifted straight from a childhood textbook. 📖 The reality is far cheekier. At its heart this is a playful, free-wheeling art form, a space where performers spin everyday stories as parody, satire and colourful jabs at rules, red tape and the people in charge. 

This time the Chomphunut Theatre Troupe reunites to give the world-famous fairy tale Cinderella a contemporary Lakhon Nok makeover, reimagining the glass-slipper princess through a distinctly Thai lens and nudging you to leave your usual assumptions at the door. 

You catch it at GalileOasis Art Space, GalileOasis on August 29, with the opening show running 1-2pm and a second at 7-8pm. Tickets cost B800 and you can book at @galileoasis.
