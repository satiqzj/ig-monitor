Brace yourself for a real medical education at @sirirajmuseum – better known to Bangkokians as the Museum of Death. Expect real anatomical specimens, evidence from murder cases and a Homo erectus skeleton, all displayed at Thailand’s oldest hospital.

📅 Wed-Mon, 10am-5pm (last admission 4pm), closed Tuesdays and public holidays
📍Siriraj Hospital, 2 Wang Lang Road, Bangkok Noi
🎟️ B100 (Thai adults), B50 (Thai children and seniors), B300 (international visitors) – one ticket covers every museum
🚫 No photos inside
