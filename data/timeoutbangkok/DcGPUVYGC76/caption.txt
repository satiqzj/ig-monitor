So, did you make it to the last one? 🧘

A few hundred people rock up to do nothing together each time, and now 'Do F**ing Nothing' returns for a third outing on September 5 and 6, 5pm to 6pm at Playground 1, Rod Fai Park. Arrive 15 minutes early, claim a spot and settle in. At 5pm everyone goes quiet, no clock-watching required, and the national anthem marks the end of the stillness. 

Fancy company after? Save the poster, raise your hand high with it showing and wait for someone to mirror you, then ask 'sit and talk together?' Groups of five to ten work best.

Stretch out, nap or wander off whenever. You've nothing to prove and the choice is always yours. ✌️

When: September 5 and 6, 5pm to 6pm
Where: Playground 1, Rod Fai Park
