The Silent Film Festival returns to Thailand for its 10th edition, bringing classic titles from across the globe back to the big screen, scored live by musicians who make the pre-sound era sing.

This year the line-up welcomes Stephen Horne, a celebrated British pianist and one of the world's finest silent-film accompanists, who last charmed local crowds in 2015. He performs across four programmes, each with its own mood and rhythm.

Even better? Admission is completely free, and every screening comes with English and Thai subtitles. Catch it over two weekends, on August 29-30 at Sala Cinema Theatre and September 5-6 at Nang Loeng Cinema.
