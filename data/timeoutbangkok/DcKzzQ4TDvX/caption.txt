Two months only!

Silpa Bhirasri National Museum has reopened for two months, with a free exhibition tracing Rattanakosin’s history through the work of Silpa Bhirasri, the father of modern Thai art. The Hall of Master Sculptures is also open for viewing for the first time. 

📍 Silpa Bhirasri National Museum and the Hall of Master Sculptures 
🗓️ Until September 20 2026
⏰ Wed-Sun, 9am-4pm
🎟️ Free entry
