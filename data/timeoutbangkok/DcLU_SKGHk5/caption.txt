A conversation in the language of jazz, told through the flick of a brush 🎷🖌

Ever wondered what jazz looks like when someone paints it? That's the whole idea behind 'Jazz Conversation', a new show from artist Kornrawee Trakarnratanakul that turns the rhythm of a late-night jam session straight onto the canvas.

Each work captures the spirit, memories and experiences of musicians mid-improvisation, with loose, expressive brushstrokes standing in for the sound. Distorted forms tangle with lines and colour, translating those musical vibrations to something you can actually see.

It's a lovely reminder that no note lives alone – jazz happens through listening, responding and letting go within the shape of a melody. Raw, honest and easy to get swept up in.

Catch it at Joyman Gallery from today until September 6. Free entry 🎨
