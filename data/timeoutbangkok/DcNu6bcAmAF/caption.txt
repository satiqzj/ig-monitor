Reckon Thailand's electronic scene deserves a bigger audience? So does 'ThaiBeats'. The new project pushes homegrown electronic music out to clubs and festivals overseas, with a fresh line-up of local acts.

Run by Bangkok party crew @dudesweetnation with support from the Department of Cultural Promotion and the Ministry of Culture, the whole thing opens submissions nationwide, gathers more than 380 tracks and settles on a final 12. Those names then land on the 'ThaiBeats Vinyl Compilation', a record posted straight to DJs and industry types abroad, giving producers who mix cultural roots with a modern outlook a genuine shot at being heard.
