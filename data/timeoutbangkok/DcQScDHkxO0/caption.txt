Some names stay with you long after the person has gone. On August 19, news breaks that Ray MacDonald has passed aged 49, and for anyone who grew up in Thailand in the 90s, that name means something.

Maybe you know Ray from Teen Talk, the youth show where he larks about on a weekday afternoon after school. Maybe it’s his films and the hitman he plays in Fun Bar Karaoke, or his parts in O-Negative, Dear Galileo and Phobia 2. Or maybe he‘s the mate you never actually meet, the one who takes you along on his travel programmes and his YouTube channel Rayron, showing you the places you only ever see on a screen.

Across nearly three decades he is, by turns, the shy boy, the monk, the graphic designer and the traveller. Time Out looks back at 14 of his roles, from his first film to his last, and each one is worth another watch.
