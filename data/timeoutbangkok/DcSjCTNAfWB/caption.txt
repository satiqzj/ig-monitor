If you have been keeping tabs on Thai electronic pop band @kikidoyoumind, you already know how big this year is. Fresh off a slot at Japan's Fuji Rock Festival on July 26, they add another headline date, the Formula 1 Singapore Airlines Singapore Grand Prix 2026.

They share the bill with Lana Del Rey, The Killers, Janet Jackson, JJ Lin, Mark Ronson and DJ Snake, with more names to come. The weekend runs October 9-11 and KIKI plays on October 10. 

Proper milestone for a group this early on, and a sign of young Thai artists reaching listeners far from home. From a mountain festival field to the streets of Singapore months later.
