Breaking news for Fujii Kaze fans. His Prema World Tour date in Bangkok, set for December 26 over the holidays, is off. An unexpected issue is behind it and no one's saying what went wrong. Loads of you have been counting down since tickets dropped.

Live Nation Tero and HEHN RECORDS have apologised to everyone who bought one, and here's how to get your money back:

- Paid by credit or debit card? No action needed. It returns to your card within two billing cycles.

- Paid another way? Take your proof of purchase to any of the 11 main @thaiticketmajor branches from August 28 to October 31, 2026, during opening hours.

Fujii Kaze and the team hope to come back soon. Otherwise, ring @thaiticketmajor on 02-262-3456 or email cs@thaiticketmajor.com.
