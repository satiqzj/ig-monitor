Ever wondered what royalty ate for dinner a hundred years ago? 🕰️

The Bank of Thailand Learning Center fills a grand old room at Bang Khun Phrom Palace with painstakingly made replica dishes, each one modelled on the food once served to the people who lived here. You get a close look at how the kitchen worked and the refined, unhurried life that went with it. 🥘 Take it slow and read all the little labels, because that's where the good stuff hides.

Spots are capped at 60 a session, so it's worth arriving early.

📍 Bang Khun Phrom Palace Open Saturday and Sunday, two sittings daily at 10.30am and 2.30pm. Free admission.
