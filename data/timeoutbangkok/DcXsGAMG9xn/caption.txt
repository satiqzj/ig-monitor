Bangkok’s feel-good fair is back 🎠🎡 

The Red Cross Fair returns to Lumphini Park for 14 days of regional Thai food, fairground rides, classic games, bargain shopping and prizes. Come hungry, try your luck and indulge in a little childhood nostalgia – all for a good cause.
