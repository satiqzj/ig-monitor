Every big city has a scene that only gets going once the shops close, and in Bangkok plenty of it runs on four wheels 🏎️ 

On September 6, 2026 that whole world comes to Terminal 21 Rama 3 for Bangkok Underground Riverside, the biggest meet the area sees all year.
The theme is 'The Fast Tokyo Drift', headlined by Beer Baiyoke and the Top Secret Team, whose RX-7 and 180SX in Liberty Walk kits are the only pair of their kind here. Modified street machines fill the rows, loud before you spot them.

Got your own build? 🔧 Park up and show it off. 

Owners drive in from across the country for evenings like this, so you're in good company. Entry is free. See you on the tarmac, September 6, midday-9pm at Terminal 21 Rama 3.
