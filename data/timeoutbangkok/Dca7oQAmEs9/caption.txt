The Ministry of Transport has 50 road projects on the go across Thailand, and a handful of the big ones are almost here.

First up is the M6 Bang Pa-In–Pak Chong motorway, which opens for free weekend trials soon and shaves a chunk off the drive to Khao Yai. Coming later, the M82 Bang Khun Thian–Ban Phaeo motorway and the Rama 3–Dao Khanong Expressway open over the next few years.

So if you love a road trip or head out of town often, save this one and give it a proper look later.
