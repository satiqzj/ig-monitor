Thailand, you’re a loved-up lot 🇹🇭🥰❤️

Thailand tops Ipsos’ Love Life Satisfaction Index 2026 with a score of 86 out of 100 – the highest among 29 countries and well above the global average of 73. Ipsos surveyed 23,268 adults from Dec 24, 2025 to Jan 9, 2026.

Indonesia follows at 85, with Mexico and Spain tied for third at 81. Japan and South Korea rank lower down the table.

Among married or partnered Thais, 95% are satisfied with their relationship, including 54% who are ‘very satisfied’. Another 86% say they feel loved, up five percentage points from last year, while 78% are satisfied with their romantic and sex lives.

Source: Ipsos Love Life Satisfaction Index 2026; 23,268 adults across 29 countries surveyed Dec 24, 2025-Jan 9, 2026.
