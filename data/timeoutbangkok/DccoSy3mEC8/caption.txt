In under a year Dib Bangkok has built a reputation at home and abroad, and picked up a spot on TIME magazine's World's Greatest Places 2026 list along the way. 

‘Giving Life’ is a new exhibition of more than 160 works by 33 Thai and international artists, running from September 26 to May 3, 2027.

Alexander Calder is the big draw. The American rethinks what a sculpture can be, making once-static objects move and respond to wind, gravity and the space around them. He stands as one of the founders of Kinetic Art and a real turning point in modern art.

The pull of the show is not only a world-famous name landing in Thailand. It also puts his work in conversation with Thai and Asian art, alongside 32 others.

Giving Life' runs from September 26-May 3, 2027 at Dib Bangkok. The museum opens Thursday to Monday, 10am to 7pm, and closes on Tuesdays and Wednesdays
