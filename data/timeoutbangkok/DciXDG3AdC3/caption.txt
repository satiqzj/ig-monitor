Hop off the BTS and you’ll find The Mesh (@themeshbangkok), a sports bar where you can dine, drink and play from morning to midnight. Breakfast can turn into brunch, dinner can turn into drinks and nobody has to call it a night early. Designed as a dining and social space, this restaurant and bar has plenty to keep you fed and entertained all day long.

Not an early bird? No problem. Swing by anytime for the all-day breakfast menu whenever you’re ready to tackle the day.

And if deciding what to eat is always your biggest dilemma, The Mesh has plenty to choose from, with Thai, Italian, Indian and Western dishes all on offer. One minute you’re munching on an artisan charcuterie board, the next you’re tucking into Pad Thai Chai Yai with crispy soft-shell crab.

Once the food coma starts to kick in, settle into their laid-back hang-out space and let the games begin. Challenge your mates to a game of pool, take aim at the darts board or break out a classic board game. For those that prefer to sit back and relax, catch the latest sporting action on the massive screens. Come Thursday and Friday, live bands take over.

There’s always a reason to celebrate at The Mesh, with seasonal festivities ranging from Oktoberfest to Christmas, plus plenty of space for your own special occasions, from birthday bashes to casual social gatherings. 

Open daily from 6am to 1am, The Mesh is ready whenever you are – whether that means breakfast before work, an afternoon bite, after-work drinks or a late-night game of pool.
