Bangkok's newest attraction has 540 smells – including whale vomit 👃

Forget top notes and dry-downs: @karmakamet's free SCENTORIUM at Erawan Bangkok lines up 540 smells from around the world, from the familiar to the downright bizarre. Baby skin, cat urine, whale vomit, dog fur, beaver musk, banknotes and medical equipment all make the cut.

A short personality quiz then builds your scent profile and sends you hunting among all 540 smells for your match.

📍Karmakamet Erawan, Erawan Bangkok
🕒 Tue-Sun, 10am-6pm
💸 Free entry
📅 Advance booking required: @karmakamet
