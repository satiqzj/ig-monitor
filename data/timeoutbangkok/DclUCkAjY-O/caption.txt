If your ideal weekend involves eating out until you can barely move, @thestandardgrillbangkok has your number.

The restaurant serves its Weekend Roast every Saturday and Sunday – that great American ritual of gravy, groaning platters and a well-earned afternoon of good company, reworked for the whole table.

Lead with the mains, served sharing-style and piled high: silky smoked salmon crudo, porchetta with shatteringly crisp crackling, and black angus picanha seared to a deep, juicy char, plus tender lamb and chicken – all begging to be passed around. Round it out with crunchy fresh salads, warm pull-apart artisanal bread, cloud-soft mash, snappy grilled broccolini and a rich, buttery mushroom fricassée.

Then, save room! Melt into velvety chocolate pudding and a butterscotch tart with a crisp, golden shell. And what's a roast without something in the glass? Settle in with a bold red or a crisp cocktail, kick back, and let the afternoon drift.

It runs every Saturday and Sunday 12pm-3pm, B1,650++ per adult and B825++ per child. Thirsty? A drinks add-on of cocktails, wines and beer starts from B1,500++
