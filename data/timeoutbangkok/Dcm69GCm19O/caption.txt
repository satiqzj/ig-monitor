Turns out growing up rarely comes with clear answers 🎬

This Friday, Doc Day Afternoon brings you two short documentaries on exactly that, 'CHEMKIDS' from Germany and 'TRAIN ROBBERS' from Norway. Both follow young people through friendship, loneliness and the gap between generations, each shaped by a different era.

Once the credits roll, stay on for a chat with 'Pa Mol' Thicha Nanakorn about youth, the in-between years and why a fair shot matters.

It's on at the Film Screening Room inside Bangkok City Library on September 4 at 4pm. Thai subtitles run throughout, entry costs nothing and only 30 seats are going, so arrive early. Register at @documentary_club 🎟️
