Clear the calendar and charge your headphones, because @lalalalisa_m has news. This September the star, Lalisa Manobal, kicks off her next solo era with a fresh six-track EP that puts Thailand front and centre.

'Press Play' arrives October 23, the 29-year-old's follow-up to her 2025 debut album 'Alter Ego'. First out is pre-release single 'SaWaDiKa' on September 3, its title borrowed from the Thai greeting 'sawatdee ka'. Anyone who spots the cryptic teasers or the giant 'Hello Sawadika' billboards around Bangkok already knows the theme. For fans who go back to her trainee days in Seoul, this homecoming counts for a lot.
