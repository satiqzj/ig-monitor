What’s a Jazz Kissa? 

We explore the world of Japanese jazz cafés and their influence on Bangkok following the eyes of  historian and documentarian Katsumasa Kusunose and his new book Jazz Kissa: The Soul of Japanese Listening Culture. 

More than places to listen to music – they are spaces for deep focus and the ultimate expression of simply being yourself, and we’re seeing more pop up in this part of the world too. Prompting us to talk to Katsumasa Kusunose himself and the team behind the book – Ted Gushue (champion of print), and Devon Turnbull (architect of sound), at SIWILAI ☕️✨
