Coffee lovers, get it in the diary. Thailand Coffee Fest is back, and it's the biggest of its kind in Southeast Asia. 

This year's theme, 'Made by All of Us', nods to a simple truth. No cup is the work of one hand, and farmers, roasters, baristas and the rest of us who drink it all play a part. It runs at IMPACT Exhibition Center in Muang Thong Thani from September 10-13, with 380-plus exhibitors, cupping tables and workshops tracing bean to cup. Here's what to look out for:

Coffee Arena – the Thailand Coffee Championships 2027, where baristas compete to represent the country abroad

Coffee Around the World – roasters and champions swapping methods and stories

Coffee People by SCATH – a networking corner for the local community

Coffee Stage – experts on the trends shaping what you'll drink next

Coffee & More Village – 400-plus stalls of cafés, roasteries, tea, matcha and bakeries

Thailand Coffee Fest 2026 runs September 10-13 at IMPACT Exhibition Center, Halls 5-8, Muang Thong Thani.
