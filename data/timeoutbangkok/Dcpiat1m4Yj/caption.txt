Anyone who caught Beyond the Rainbow will be glad to hear it's back. @eyedropperfill's gentle art show, made for people who've lost a pet, returns to @rivercitycontemporary , the arts complex by the Chao Phraya, from September 18 to November 22.

The angle shifts a little this year, centring on an idea the makers call 'Growing Together'. We assume we're the ones doing the caring, but the exhibition makes the opposite case, that our animals have looked after us all along.

The best bit? A room where real pets come too. Dogs and cats only, mind, and they sit in a stroller or an enclosed carrier the whole way through.

Tickets start at B150, or B200 with a small companion. Grab yours at @rivercitycontemporary’s link in bio.
