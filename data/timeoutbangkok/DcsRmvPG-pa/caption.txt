Bang Na has a new headliner. Happitat opens on August 21, 2026, which is the World's First Happiness Destination

The Grand Celebration on August 21 took a 'Butterfly Phenomenon' theme, with a Green Carpet hosting Zhou Yiran, Chompoo Araya's family, Freen Sarocha and Butterbear & Bianca, before 2,222 drones lit the sky in the country's biggest-ever display.

At Happitat, start at the Grand Stairs, home to the largest and highest-resolution indoor digital screen in Southeast Asia, spanning 385sqm and running two immersive shows. Point your phone at it for 'The Butterfly Trail' and rare butterflies land on your screen, or wake long-extinct wildlife in ‘Forest of Time’. Over at the Whimsical Market you order a coffee under a tree as tall as a three-storey building, with more than 60 cafés and restaurants to work through and a light and sound show to catch.

Bring the kids, the parents, the grandparents and the dog too. Every generation gets a reason to show up here, whether you come to eat, play, explore or just spend proper time together. And when everyone needs a breather, pocket gardens sit on every floor, with more than 48,000sqm of natural forest right behind the project.

If you want to know how it’s like, go and find your own kind of happiness 🦋
