The @bangkok_bma opens the first phase of the Rajavithi Skywalk, the biggest overhaul of this part of the city in two decades. The new stretch finally closes that gap and completes the circle.

The new Rajavithi–Phahonyothin stretch measures roughly 1.3km, from the monument to Tuek Chai Intersection, and connects straight to the BTS. It also joins up the existing overpasses, so the whole route works as one system rather than a run of dead ends.

The clever part is where it goes. The path reaches five hospitals at the heart of the medical district, among them Rajavithi, Phramongkutklao and the Hospital for Tropical Diseases. Get off the train and you walk the rest above the traffic. At 4 metres across and clear of planters, wheelchairs and hospital beds pass easily. Four lifts run the length, with tactile paving throughout.

The first phase opens in early October, with more to follow.
