The moon swings round again, and @intercontinentalbangkok knows exactly what you want to be clutching when it does. This year, the hotel serves up its 2026 mooncake collection as a proper looker – deep royal purple, laced with turquoise lattice, finished with gold embossing that catches the light like a wink across the dinner table. The circular patterns nod to the full moon, the swirls to togetherness, the gold to a bit of prosperity.

Inside sit four flavours that flirt with tradition and win. There's silky custard cream for the purists, then durian with salted egg yolk for the brave, lotus seed with salted egg yolk for the classicists and osmanthus lotus with salted egg yolk for the connoisseurs. Boxes come in sixes and eights, nestled in jewel-toned compartments that make gifting feel almost smug.

Grab a box from now through September 2026, priced at B1,088 for six and B1,448 for eight. Ring 02 656 0444, email mooncake.ICBKK@IHG.com or visit bangkok.intercontinental.com.
