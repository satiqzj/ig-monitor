Annyeonghaseyo! Kongju Korean Restaurant (@kongjukoreanrestaurant) is back with a refreshed space and menu, marking a new chapter in its 30-year history of serving Korean cuisine in Bangkok. While the restaurant has had a makeover, its heart remains the same: traditional Korean food inspired by royal cuisine, with a contemporary touch.

The return brings a new signature: the Australian-raised Hanwoo, a first-of-its-kind offering in Thailand. Combining Korea’s distinctive premium beef with Australia’s farming practices, this multinational affair promises rich marbling and a melt-in-your-mouth tenderness. 

Add the cheese pork ribs for a little cheese-pull competition, then satisfy those late-night K-drama cravings with sizzling stone-pot rice bowls and incredibly fluffy steamed eggs that make the perfect banchan. For classic Korean bites to pair with drinks, don’t miss the seafood pajeon and dak gangjeong either. Because if we’re honest, there’s no such thing as ordering just one dish. 

A new space, new dishes and plenty of new favourites to discover at Kongju Korean Restaurant, Pathumwan Princess Hotel, with the same authentic taste that has kept it going for over three decades. Open daily from 11.30am-2pm and 5.30-10pm.
