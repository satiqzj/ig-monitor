Eleven days, films from 113 countries and not a single baht to pay at the door. What the Doc Film Festival (WTD!) 2026 is back, and the promise is a simple one. Every screening is free, start to finish.

WTD! 2026 runs September 3-13 at Doc Club & Friends in the Cloud 11 building (Punnawithi BTS)and Century Theater Sukhumvit (On Nut BTS).

Admission is free, subtitles are sorted and you collect your ticket at the venue 30 minutes before each screening. 

For the full programme, head to @whatthedocfilmfestival
