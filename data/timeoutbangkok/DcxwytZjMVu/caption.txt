When it comes to a present for friends and family, little beats a good box of mooncakes. If your desk has started collecting a few, you know exactly what time of year it is. Every Mid-Autumn Festival, Bangkok trades them between family, friends, colleagues and business partners, and the season is properly underway.

At Breeze by lebua (@breezebangkok), the ritual arrives as a collection from executive chef Sam Pang and dim sum chef He Shui Zhen. The classics stay close, while bringing in three new flavours. Signature custard, durian yellow bean paste and emperor five nut with Jinhua ham hold to tradition, while matcha pistachio, chocolate lava and dragon fruit white lotus paste with egg yolk do something different. The standout highlight is the Dragon Fruit White Lotus Paste with Egg Yolk – a vibrant and refreshing interpretation of the traditional mooncake.

At the launch, a Mooncake Workshop puts you next to both chefs to put a batch together yourself. The fillings come ready, so you sit a salted egg yolk in the centre, seal the dough around it, then press the ball in a carved wooden mould until the pattern comes out clean. Knock it three times – left, right and down – and out comes your very own mooncake.

The box is part of the gift, too. One collection comes in a long case shaped like lebua tower, built to keep long after the last mooncake goes. You can pick from six signature mooncake box with tea at B2,888, another six in the tower box at B2,188 or a two-piece box at B788.
