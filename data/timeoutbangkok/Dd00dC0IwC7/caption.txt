Flying to or from Bangkok amid the floods? Thai Airways, AirAsia, Bangkok Airways, Nok Air, Thai Lion Air and Thai Vietjet have options for eligible bookings. The airports are open, but road access and some flights have been affected. Check your airline’s dates and conditions, then allow extra time to get there.
