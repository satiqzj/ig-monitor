Clear a shelf, Bangkok, because @bigbadwolfbooks_th. Thailand’s biggest book sale returns to Platinum Pop from October 2-13, and for its tenth anniversary it brings more than 1 million books at up to 95 percent off.

Expect children’s picture books, cookbooks, photobooks, design tomes and translated fiction, plus unsold stock from independent publishers abroad. Collectors should head straight for the merchandise section, where Game of Thrones board games, special-edition box sets and Harry Potter wizard’s chess sets are up for grabs.

Doors open 10am until midnight daily and entry is free. Bring a wheeled suitcase, wear comfy shoes and go on a weekday evening to skip the queues at the tills.
