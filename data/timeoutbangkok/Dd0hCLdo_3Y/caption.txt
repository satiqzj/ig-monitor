It's been a rough few days in the capital. More than 330mm of rain fell on parts of the city between Thursday and Sunday, and on September 26 the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA) declared all 50 districts disaster areas. Around 52,000 households are affected and canal levels remain high.

Nearly 4,900 people now sleep in temporary shelters, mostly temples and schools. In Kheha Romklao, families bed down on classroom floors with the desks pushed aside, dogs curled up in the hallways.

If you need to leave home, open sites are listed by district here. The BMA hotline, 1555, runs 24 hours. Stay safe, everyone.
