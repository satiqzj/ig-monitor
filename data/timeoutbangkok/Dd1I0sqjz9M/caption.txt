The 28-year wait is over! Thailand’s eventing squad takes team 🥇 at the Aichi-Nagoya 2026 Asian Games, its first since Bangkok 1998. 

Third before the final day, the riders post three clear rounds on the cross-country course in Tokyo, hold their score at 109.90 and overtake hosts Japan to top the podium. Woraphat Sittiju and Uster de Chanay add individual silver too, and the win is Thailand’s fifth gold in Japan.
