The beach is back 🏝️

Thailand’s famous Maya Bay is reopening on October 1 after its annual conservation break. The Phi Phi Leh paradise, as seen in Leonardo DiCaprio’s The Beach, is ready for another season of turquoise waters, dramatic cliffs and chill days by the beach.
