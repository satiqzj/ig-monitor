Bangkok is keeping the burners busy. Noisette (@noisettebkk) brings seasonal French cooking and a childhood friendship to Yen Akat, while Aunglo’s new sibling Krobnok by Yangrak (@krobnok.by.yangrak) goes all in on crispy Thai dishes on Decho Road.

In Chinatown, Restaurant ROSA (@rosachinatown) serves a tasting menu through words and clues. At Central Park, Age.3 (@age.3_bangkok) makes its Thai debut with fried shokupan sandwiches filled with whipped cream or savoury ingredients, including Thailand-only Thai tea matcha brûlée and pad kra pao.

For a rather more extravagant catch-up, Stéphanie Le Quellec and Vincent Thierry reunite at Chef’s Table by lebua (@chefs_table_bangkok) for a seven-course collaboration on October 1-3.

Five reasons to loosen the belt. Let’s get into it.
