Ruby’s (@rubys.bkk) is Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok’s 16-seat cocktail bar that tells a real story, one glass at a time. Broken hearts, the twists of fate tied to precious stones and that very human urge for revenge shape the menu, with every serve mixed by skilled bartenders under soft, low light.

This October 1, Ruby’s launches Volume 2 of its menu with 13 new drinks, each inspired by a different gem. Every one reveals another side of the tale that began when the doors opened in 2024.

Volume 1 ends with love gone wrong. Titled ‘Transformation’, the sequel picks up from there, trading payback for healing, restoration and figuring out who you are afterwards.

The room stays as it is, so you sit close enough to the bartender stirring your order to hear where it fits in the plot. Pick one, ask what it means, then get another to find out what happens next.

Space is tight, so book ahead.

Ruby’s is open daily from 5.30pm to midnight. For reservations, call 02 056 9999 or email Rubys.KimptonMaaLai@ihg.com

Diamond
Pale gold, with a hand-cut ice pyramid echoing the uncut Cullinan diamond. Tequila, mezcal, sake, vermouth, rooibos and pear are milk-clarified for a light, floral, faintly smoky sip.

Jadeite
Glowing green and lit from below, with gold-flecked ikura resting on the rim. Japanese whisky, matcha liqueur, yuzu, lychee and wasabi cordial bring a sharp kick, softened by salty roe.

Fire Opal
Named after a Mexican gem and served ablaze. Tortilla-infused tequila, Thai tea chilli liqueur, white cacao, green pepper, habanero and agave make it spicy and earthy, with chocolate underneath.

Sea Pearl
A maritime vesper of Tito’s vodka, Malfy Gin con Limone and Mancino Vermouth Secco, plus dill, finger lime and fat-washed oyster. Dry, briny and born from grit, like any pearl.

Amethyst
Deep ruby and milk-punch clarified, with a seasoned rim made for licking. Islay gin, Lanna vodka, açai, vanilla, butterfly pea and citrus keep it berry-sweet and refreshing.
