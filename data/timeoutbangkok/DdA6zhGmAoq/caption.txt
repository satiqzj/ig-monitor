Bangkok’s Royal Barge crews spend weeks rehearsing on the Chao Phraya, and you can watch it all for free. The oarsmen set off from Rama VIII Bridge towards Wat Arun at 3pm on September 8, 17 and 23, with more across October. The two full-dress runs on October 21 and 28 come closest to the real thing, decorated barges and all, without the crowds.

Grab a spot on either bank between Krung Thon Bridge and Wat Arun. The riverside park by Siriraj Hospital and the pier at Wat Rakhang cover the Thonburi side, while Santichaiprakan Park, Tha Maharaj and Tha Chang sit opposite. The Wat Arun end is the postcard shot; upriver, you see more of the fleet.

Practice continues until the royal Kathin robe-offering ceremony on November 6, marking Her Majesty the Queen’s fourth-cycle birthday. Dates and river closures change, so check official announcements first.
