Bangkok bookworms, clear some shelf space 📚

Book Expo Thailand 2026 – the 31st National Book Fair – returns to QSNCC from October 23-November 1, after the previous edition drew a record 1.5 million visitors and more than THB 474 million in sales.

This year’s ‘Unread Messages’ edition packs seven zones into ten days, covering fiction, comics, BL/yaoi, children’s books, second-hand finds and foreign-language titles.

📍 Halls 5-7, LG Floor, @qsncc
 🗓️ October 23-November 1
 ⏰ 10am-9pm daily
 🚇 MRT QSNCC, Exit 3
 🎟️ Free entry
ℹ️ Full programme: @thaibookfair
