You might know Idris Elba best as Luther, but he's just as comfortable behind a set of decks, having DJ'd since his teens in London. 

This September the British actor, rapper and producer brings his Sound International show to Bangkok for one night, joined by @wildealer and @elliekhodayar, two names pushing Thailand's fast-growing house scene. 

Doors open at 7.45pm, so wear something you can move in and expect a late one.

Sound International x Bangkok plays Cloud 11 Theatre on Saturday September 26 2026. Tickets cost B2,690 and are on sale now.
