Fancy a weekend by the water that offers more than just a gig or a gallery? Pin down the L'On Art Festival, the first from the team behind @l.onbangkok, running from September 10-13 at Yodpiman River Walk, next to the Pak Khlong Talat flower market.

Across four days, the riverfront fills with painters at their easels, live music and performances, food stalls and a wide spread of Thai drinks, boozy and alcohol-free. Order something cold, watch the sun drop over the Chao Phraya and stay put until the lights come up.

Part of every ticket helps the Children's Hospital Foundation, supporting premature babies, children fighting cancer and those with cerebral disabilities. A proper night out that also does some good.

The festival is at Yodpiman River Walk, Sep 10-13, 3pm-midnight. Tickets are B105.
