Last year Fungjai turned 10 with ‘Fungjai Jud 2025’, a reunion of acts from every era of Bangkok's music scene. Now the crew is back with a fresh one called ‘Fungjai Mun’, built on the same idea of gathering very different acts for a single night, only turned up louder.

Eight acts and a DJ share the bill: BLVCKHEART, Mirrr, WAV, DOTDOTDOT, Television Off, The Richman Toy, Jazz Spooknick Papillon Kook Kook and NAP THE NAP, with DJ Bookythestrider (of Patibudkarn Nuemek) spinning between every set so the room never goes quiet.

The whole thing happens on October 10 at Idea Live, Bravo BKK. Tickets start at B890 for early birds to B1,590on the door.
