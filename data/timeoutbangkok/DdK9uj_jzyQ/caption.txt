Turns out you're not the only one who keeps rebooking a Thai holiday. New @agodathailand accommodation figures for the first half of 2026 put Bangkok second in Asia for the cities travellers return to most.

Closer to home, Bangkok, Phuket, Pattaya, Chiang Mai and Hat Yai hold the top five spots for repeat visits for the second year running, each finishing exactly where it did last year.

The capital also picks up the crown as the world's greatest city for nightlife in 2026, per Time Out's Best Cities for Nightlife with Intrepid Travel. The ranking draws on surveys of locals worldwide plus takes from Time Out writers. 

Up from 13th in 2025, we now sit at number one, ahead of a long roll call of established party towns. Berlin, New York City, Melbourne and São Paulo make up the rest of the top five.
