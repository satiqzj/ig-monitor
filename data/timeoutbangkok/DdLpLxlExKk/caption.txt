Bangkok’s newest contemporary art museum has made quite the entrance. 🎨

In less than a year, Dib Bangkok has gone from an ambitious new arrival to one of the most talked-about names on the city’s cultural scene – and director Dr. Miwako Tezuka has been there helping shape what comes next.

Ahead of the opening of Giving Life, a sprawling new exhibition bringing together more than 160 works by 33 Thai and international artists, Time Out sat down with Miwako to talk art, Bangkok and building a museum from the ground up.

Why Thailand? Why now? And can Bangkok become one of the world’s great contemporary art cities?
