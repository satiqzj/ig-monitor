Punk safety pins, Orb rings and four decades of archive jewellery – Vivienne Westwood’s touring exhibition has landed in Thailand 👑 

Bangkok is the fifth stop after Wellington, Shanghai, Chengdu and Macau. Across eight themed rooms, archive and runway pieces sit beside complete catwalk looks, while Galaxy Room, created with Microsoft, turns your selections into a personalised digital constellation ✨
