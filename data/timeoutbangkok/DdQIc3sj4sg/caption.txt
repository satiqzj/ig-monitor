Good news for anyone who spends their weekends down the front at a gig, hunting for a new favourite band. Bangkok Music City returns for two days on January 30-31 2027, and the full line-up is finally here. 

Thai musicians share the bill with acts from Réunion, France, Indonesia, the Netherlands, Japan, Germany, Vietnam, Mongolia and Malaysia, which makes for a properly global couple of nights. The whole thing centres on one idea, 'Discover Asian Music's Future', and it puts the region's rising talent in front of crowds well outside Thailand.
