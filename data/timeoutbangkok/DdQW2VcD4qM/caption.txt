Did you know you can take an organised trip from Bangkok to Ayutthaya for free?

KOH KERD includes return transport, a traditional Khon performance, the Arts of the Kingdom Museum and transport between the cultural stops – all for free thanks to TAT (@tourismthailand).

Our intrepid intern tried it for herself to see what you actually get, how the day works and whether it's worth booking. Spoiler alert: It’s absolutely brilliant.

KOH KERD runs Wednesday to Sunday until December 26, 2026, with advance reservation required.

Tap the link in @timeoutbangkok’s bio for the full experience and details on how to book.
