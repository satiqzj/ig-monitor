Do you spend more on your dog or cat than on yourself? Then bookmark this one. @prynwan.th is a brand made for pet people, stocking all sorts of essentials, little outfits and cute bits you’ll want to take home the second you spot them.

Right now it hosts the Play Marché pop-up at @centralworld, a curated market of lifestyle products and treats put together with dog and cat lovers in mind.

Here are some of the highlights worth turning up for:
- Play Marché: a collection of dog toys dreamed up around the market theme, all in Prynwan’s playful signature style.
- DOGISGOD collection: a capsule that reads the bond between humans and their dogs through tee, tote, cap, mug and coin pouch.
- Prynwan: the crowd favourites, ready to mix and match and brought straight from the main store.

There’s a bit of fun to be had too. Snap a photo at the ‘Play Marché in My Basket’ Polaroid activity, share it on social media and you’ll bag a few special privileges plus a free Polaroid to take home. It runs from September 15-30 2026.

Prynwan Play Marché is on now until October 27 at centralwOrld, Center Court, second floor, Zone F. Daily 10am-10pm.
