Good news for anyone who plans their week around the cinema. VIPA International Film Festival 2026 is back for 15 days of films from all over the world, and it makes room for the stuff you usually miss, chats with cast and crew and Q&As that carry on after the credits.

The big draw is the One Cut strand, five films shot in a single unbroken take. Watching each director attempt the same trick in a different way is a rare treat if you care how they get made.

Between screenings you also get workshops, panel talks and exhibitions from artists in all sorts of fields.

VIPA International Film Festival 2026 runs September 11-25 at Thai PBS, Vibhavadi Rangsit Road. Follow @vipadotme for the full line-up.
