A temple where cats outnumber the monks 🐈

Beyond the famous Reclining Buddha, did you know that Wat Pho is also home to around 200 cats?

Spot them roaming the grounds, napping by the chedis and happily posing for passing tourists in exchange for an ear scratch. 

We joined Venerable Monk Mahanalin  on his evening rounds to meet Bangkok’s most unexpected temple residents, and find a bit of furry wisdom along the way.
