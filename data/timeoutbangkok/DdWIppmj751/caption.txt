Three Bangkok hotels have officially cracked the world’s top 10, according to The 50 Best list. But how come? 🏨🌏

The newly revealed The 50 Best Hotels 2026 puts Capella Bangkok at No.2, Four Seasons Bangkok at Chao Phraya River at No.3 and Mandarin Oriental Bangkok at No.10. 

Aman Nai Lert made it to No.48, making that four in the top 50 and five in the top 100, including Rosewood Bangkok at No.70. 

A huge win for Bangkok hospitality – but with the ranking facing growing scrutiny over how its ‘best’ hotels are actually chosen, just how much should we read into the numbers? 👀
