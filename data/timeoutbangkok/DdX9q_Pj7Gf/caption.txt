Banjos and fiddles have a small but devoted following in this city, and this October they get their biggest date yet. Southeastern Gathering returns for a third year, run by Trad Bangkok, the crew behind Bluegrass Underground and the local Irish sessions.

The talent travels a long way. Tray Wellington flies over from the US fresh off a 2024 Steve Martin Banjo Prize. Tokyo sends Bluegrass Police. Irish string players Luke Coffey and Cameron Mather sign on alongside a week of local acts.

Pick your format: a 20-seat penthouse session where you sit close enough to watch the fretwork, a welcome jam with canapés and a full bar, six hours on the main stage or a Sunday brunch of live tunes.

Southeastern Gathering 2026 runs at Public House from October 20-25. Grab your tix at @bluegrassbkk’s link in bio.
