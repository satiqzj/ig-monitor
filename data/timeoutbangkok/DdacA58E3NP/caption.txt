Thailand's first immersive aquarium opens at Seacon Square Srinakarin in late January 2027, tucked inside the North Wing's MyScape section. 

Across 1,400 sqm, nine themed areas mix real marine life with light, colour, sound and some clever tech, so you can watch, touch and wander through the lot. Index Creative Village is behind it, working to the concept 'Where Nature Becomes Art'.
