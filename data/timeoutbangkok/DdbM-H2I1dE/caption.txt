Already plotting your next escape before this one wraps up? OSOTHO Fair returns for a second run, and this year the Tourism Authority of Thailand gives the weekend over to storytelling. Three days on the lawn at Museum Siam, grills going and no ticket required.

17 kitchens from Thailand's smaller cities haul their best plates to Bangkok, among them Chumphon pizza, Isan-meets-izakaya skewers, Chiang Rai khao soi and century-old halal sweets. 10 independent makers sell homeware and bags, weaving banana rope and dyeing indigo cloth by hand. Book a workshop and you can mould a Buddha figure, print a cyanotype or roll a moss ball.

The garden also holds live music, chat about life on the road, balloon-twisters for little ones and Recording Memories, an exhibition of rare black-and-white photographs and vintage adverts from OSOTHO, the country's long-running travel magazine.

OSOTHO Fair #2: The Travellers' Tales runs September 18-20, 11am-8pm, at Museum Siam (MRT Sanam Chai, exit 1). Entry is free.
