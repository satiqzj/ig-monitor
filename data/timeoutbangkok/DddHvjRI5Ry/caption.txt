Christmas in Bangkok is en pointe this year 🎄🩰✨

The Saint Petersburg National Ballet Theatre brings Tchaikovsky’s The Nutcracker to Siam Pic Hall on December 24-25 as part of its ASEAN Tour 2026, with a live orchestra and Mariinsky Theatre soloists Oksana Bondareva and Ivan Sitnikov. 

Early-bird tickets are 20% off until 40% of seats have sold. After that, regular prices apply.
