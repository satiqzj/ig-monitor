This year, the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration's Car Free festival invites everyone to change the way they get around town. Leave the car at home and walk, run, cycle, hop on a bus, catch a boat or take the train from September 20-27 instead.

The date to mark is September 22, known worldwide as World Car-Free Day. Nobody expects you to give up driving altogether, though. Skip the motor for a couple of short trips, or squeeze a few friends in and share one ride, and you're already doing your bit.

In Bangkok, it runs September 20-27. Follow @bangkok_bma for the full line-up of activities. Coming soon!
