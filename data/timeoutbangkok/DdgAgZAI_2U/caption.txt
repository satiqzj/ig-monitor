Fresh from throwing a sato festival on the banks of the Chao Phraya, the @beerpeople99 team swap the river for a driving range. 

'One Beer: Hole in One' gathers more than 50 Thai craft breweries and over 100 beers to choose from at Topgolf Megacity Bangna, so you can work through a tasting flight then step up and take a swing between rounds. 

As they put it, 'one glass could change your world forever.' Live bands and food stalls keep things going from mid-afternoon till late, with golf games on hand if you fancy some friendly competition. 

Topgolf Megacity Bangna hosts One Beer: Hole in One on Oct 3-4, 3pm-11pm. Grab tix at @beerpeople99

Getting there: take the BTS to Udom Suk, leave by exit 5 and hop on the shuttle bus to Megabangna, then the shuttle service from the Mega Terminal outdoor zone by Big C Extra straight to Topgolf.
