Bangkok International Fashion Week returns to Siam in style ✨

BIFW2026 from October 1–11, with 10 runway shows, student designers and global labels taking over Siam Paragon, Siam Center and Siam Discovery.

Catch established Thai names including FLYNOW, ISSUE, NAGARA, POEM and VVON SUGUNNASIL at Siam Paragon, then see the next generation at Siam Center and international and Vietnamese labels at Siam Discovery.

This year’s theme is ‘TH-nomenal Extraordinary: Lead the Culture, Wear the Future’. 

📍Siam Paragon | Siam Center | Siam Discovery
 🗓️ October 1–11, 2026
