Bangkok picks up another Southeast Asian first pop up this month. The Swiss house DAVIDOFF opens its debut men's leather accessories pop-up Store in the region, on the first floor of King Power Rangnam, so no need to squeeze it in before your next trip.

Two collections lead the display. MOTION COLLECTION is more expansive, contemporary pieces in Italian leather for men who want refinement without the fuss, distinguished by the signature pizzicato seam and bold custom hardware. COMPACT COLLECTION, it takes a different approach, crafted in hard-wearing Saffiano leather for the man who travels, compact enough for a jacket pocket, yet resilient enough to go the distance.
