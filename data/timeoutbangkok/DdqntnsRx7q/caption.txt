Dib Bangkok (@dibbangkok) is back with Giving Life, their latest exhibition of more than 160 works by 33 Thai and international artists.

A world-class art landmark named one of TIME’s World’s Greatest Places 2026, Dib’s curator walked us through a special pre-opening tour, enlightening us to the legendary kinetic art pioneer Alexander Calder and the influence his work has on this moving, living and breathing exhibition. 

On the second floor, follow the history of Thai art in the 1970s through more than 60 archival documents, and encounter striking interactive works including a breathing white elephant sculpture, alongside pieces by Paul Chan, Izumi Kato and Rirkrit Tiravanija.

The exhibition also features the first CHANEL Gateway Commission, Project 147 (๑๔๗) by Navin Nootong, which incorporates cutting-edge video game technology. 

Art at Dib Bangkok is not something static, but as something that is ‘alive’, moving and interacting with its audience.

Experience Giving Life for yourself at Dib Bangkok, Sukhumvit Soi 40 from September 26 2026 to May 3, 2027. Open Thursday–Monday, 10am–7pm. Closed Tuesday and Wednesday.
 
Admission: Thai nationals B550 / international visitors B700 / students B150-250. Tickets can be booked via the Dib Bangkok website.
