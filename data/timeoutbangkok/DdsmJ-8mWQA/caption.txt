Travelling by 🚆 this New Year? Mark your calendar.

The State Railway of Thailand (SRT) opens advance bookings up to 90 days ahead for journeys between December 25 and January 3 2027. Seats for December 25 go on sale on September 26 at 8.30am, via @official_srt and at railway stations.
