⚠️ FLOOD ALERT: Motorists are advised to avoid 21 roads affected by flooding following days of heavy rain across the capital.

The Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) has also advised people living near canals or in low-lying areas to move belongings and valuables to higher ground, and to check the latest flood conditions before leaving home.

Take extra care when travelling, avoid flooded roads where possible and allow additional time for journeys. 

Stay safe out there, Bangkok – and let’s hope the rain clears soon. 🌧️
