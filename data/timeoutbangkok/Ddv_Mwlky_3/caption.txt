We went to Giving Life, Dib Bangkok‘s second exhibition, and rare pieces by Alexander Calder lead the way. The 20th century American sculptor’s mobiles hang from the ceiling and turn slowly as people walk past. 

Around them, Thai and international artists offer their takes on ‘life’ and ‘movement’, from Rachel Youn‘s creations made from repurposed machines to Nawin Nuthong’s Project 147, where you pick up a controller at one of six game consoles. More than 160 works by 33 names fill the space, so we‘ll leave most for you to discover. Here are 10 masterpieces we love. If you go, tell us yours.

Dib Bangkok is open Thursday through Tuesday from 10am-7pm, and is closed on Wednesdays.
