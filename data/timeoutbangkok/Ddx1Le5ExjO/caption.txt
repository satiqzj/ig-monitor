What happens when a Bangkok hotel takes inspiration from everything outside its front door? 🏙️🌳 

At Andaz One Bangkok (@andazonebangkok), Lumphini Park’s trees, tangled electrical wires, old architecture, clock towers and even those unmistakable Bangkok pavements have all influenced the design inside.

But the idea isn’t just to bring the neighbourhood inside – it’s to send guests back out to experience it for themselves.

Join the Lumphini Green Run and you’ll head across the road for a guided morning jog through the park, learning about its history and daily life along the way. Back at the hotel, Flow takes inspiration from the park’s wonderfully public exercise culture, mixing elements of Tai Chi and energetic group aerobics into a pool-based wellness session.

Elsewhere, guests can explore the neighbourhood, take a duck boat onto the lake, spot Lumphini’s famously enormous monitor lizards or simply watch the park from the infinity pool above.

We spoke with Director of Rooms Nipapan ‘Auk’ Stapol to discover how Andaz One Bangkok’s ‘vertical neighbourhood’ connects its art, architecture, food, wellness and experiences to the city around it.
