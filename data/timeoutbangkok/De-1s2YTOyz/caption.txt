The first places that come to mind when you think of world-famous art? Paris? New York City? How about Bangkok?

For four days only, October 27-30, Dib26, a smaller, experimental sister space to Dib Bangkok, opens its doors free of charge, partnering with Christie’s to bring two masterpieces by Picasso and Matisse, alongside works by other contemporary artists. 

So, stop by while Picasso and Matisse are in town and don’t miss Muse: Beyond Human 🖼️
