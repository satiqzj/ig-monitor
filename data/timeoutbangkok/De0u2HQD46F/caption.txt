You might already know RADA’s voice. She sang young Anna in the Thai dub of Frozen 2. Now the 15-year-old is main vocalist in A2O LITE, Lee Soo-man’s new girl group, and their single ‘NEON’ hit the top of Melon’s Top 100 just 29 hours after release.

She’s not the only one. ALURE, who voiced Antonio in the Thai version of Encanto, makes his debut in 15-member boy band A2O SOUL with ‘SUPERSTAR’.

Two 🇹🇭 teens, two new groups, one very proud week.
