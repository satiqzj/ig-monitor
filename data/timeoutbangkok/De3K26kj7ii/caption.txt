Who else had ‘The Blower’s Daughter’ on repeat after their first big breakup? 

Damien Rice’s debut album O turns 25 next year, and the Irish singer-songwriter marks the occasion with a Bangkok show at KBank SiamPic Hall on February 20, 2027.

Bangkok is one of 25 cities on his anniversary world tour, which focuses on those early songs.
