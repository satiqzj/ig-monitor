This Halloween, Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok (@kimptonmaalaibangkok) gives you two good reasons to get into the spooky spirit: Bar.Yard’s (@bar.yard) Ghosts of Greatness rooftop party on October 31 and Stock.Room’s Día de los Muertos edition of El Sabor de Kimpton Brunch on November 1. 

Start on the 40th floor, where music legends make a comeback and the party is anything but dead. DJs Daddy Gold, Sornthep and Natty join a magic show and complimentary photo booth, with every excuse to dress as your favourite icon or finally wear that outfit you’ve been saving. Entry is free from 8pm-2am, with advance reservations required. Choose from three two-hour free-flow packages, starting at B990 net online for red, white and sparkling house wines, with spirits and Champagne available in the higher tiers. Food and drinks are also available à la carte.

Come Sunday, swap the spooky soundtrack for a colourful Día de los Muertos feast at Stock.Room, on the fifth floor. Mexican classics, Argentinian empanadas and Nikkei seafood join smoky grilled meats, with pan de muerto and tres leches cake for a sweet finish. Brunch runs from noon-3pm.

Book online for B1,990 net per adult with free-flow soft drinks and one margarita, or B2,990 net with free-flow alcoholic drinks, including margaritas, house wines and beers. Walk-in rates are B1,990++ and B2,990++ respectively. Round up your brunch crew for the come four, pay three offer. This is one delicious afterlife worth sticking around for.
