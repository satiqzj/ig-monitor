No plans for the last weekend of November? Think Fest returns to Ari from November 27-29, spreading more than 40 activities across five sois. 

Wander between art installations, talks, book clubs, flea markets and workshops, and keep an eye out for the secret parties tucked away along the route. This year’s theme is ‘Remember Me’, a nudge to revisit your favourite Ari moments and make a few new ones. It runs 10am-midnight daily, a short stroll from Ari BTS Exit 3.
