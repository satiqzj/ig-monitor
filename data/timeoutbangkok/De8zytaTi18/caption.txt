Last year, Viharn put a live band inside a crematorium in Suphan Buri. This Halloween, it moves to Bangkok.

On October 31, the fourth floor of 1981 Soul & Sold becomes a dim, red-lit lab built around breath and the human body. Expect real Buddhist urns holding cremated remains, visuals that shift with the sound and seven stages of experimental music from Juna Mae, Faustus, Makara, Ong TheLayers and more.

Can’t wait? The exhibition Daeng Raem Kham (Red at Dusk) runs on the first floor October 26-November 3.
