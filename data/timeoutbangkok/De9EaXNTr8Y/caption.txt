The adult sleepover of our dreams ☁️ 

White beds spread across a loft, floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Bangkok’s skyline and absolutely zero plans for the day. Everyone’s doing their own thing, whether that’s getting lost in a good book, staring out the window like the main character in an indie film or simply lying around doing sweet nothing. No awkward small talk, no pressure to be productive and definitely no ‘just one quick email’. Just you, the city views and a little permission to be gloriously lazy. And honestly, after filming this, we took the hint and did exactly the same. No work, just vibes.
