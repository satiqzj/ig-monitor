Phones off, 📚 out. Under the Tree Club is a new free reading meet-up in Lumphini Park. 

Bring a book you love, find a patch of grass under one of the park’s big rain trees and read alongside strangers doing the same. 

At the end, everyone swaps whatever stuck with them, in Thai or English, before wandering towards Silom for dinner. It kicks off on October 3, 3.30pm-5.30pm, then every first Saturday of the month. Places are capped at 30, so register via @yepacademy_kh link in bio.

📍 Lumphini Park
🗓 Every first Saturday of the month
📅 Oct 3, Nov 6, Dec 5 and Jan 7, 2027
