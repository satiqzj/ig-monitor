Say ‘Song Wat’ and you might picture weathered shophouses, Chinese signboards and sacks of dried chillies. They’re still here – along with specialty coffee, Thai food, street art, galleries and bars tucked into old buildings.

Wear comfortable shoes and give the road a full afternoon. We’ve mapped out where to eat, browse, stop for coffee and stay for a drink. Check individual opening hours before setting off.
