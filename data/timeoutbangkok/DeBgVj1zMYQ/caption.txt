Aquaman, mermaids, Tom Holland’s umbrella dance: it’s not Halloween, it’s the floods in Thailand. 🌊

When the floodwaters rise, so does the Thai sense of humour. The internet has filled with memes, costumes and wonderfully unhinged ways of making light of a very difficult situation.

But behind the viral moments, there’s another side to the story. With communities cut off by the water, volunteers are loading trucks, wading through flooded streets and delivering food to people who can’t get out.

So while we laugh along with Thailand’s flood memes, we’re also looking at the people stepping up when help is needed most.
