Still working out what to do this Sunday? Sorted. Thailand’s National Youth Poetry Slam closes Bangkok Climate Action Week on October 11, and the doors are open to everyone.

Kids from across Thailand, aged eight to 17, perform their own poems on stage in Thai, English or both. Teacher Illya Sumanto hosts the juniors and Sabrina Dcates the seniors, with music from Notep. The theme is ‘We Destine: The World We Want to Live In’, so expect sharp lines about rising water, community and hope.

Head to Lumpini Recreation Center Hall between 11am and 2pm. Bring the family, a bottle of water for the heat and a bit of patience for first-time nerves.
