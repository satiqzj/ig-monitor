Ask a Bangkok petrolhead where they spend their weekends and you’ll probably hear about a meet-up on the top deck of a mall multi-storey or a late-night cruise along the expressway. Grey importer B Autohaus, best known for its base on Vibhavadi-Rangsit Road, now wants a spot on that list with The Autonomy, a new hangout for anyone who knows their torque from their horsepower.

The big draw is C-Stack, run by pre-owned specialist C-Certified and billed as southeast Asia’s longest vertical showroom. Stand at the bottom, tip your head back and you’ll see polished bonnets lined up floor after floor overhead.

Around it sits a hand-picked marketplace alongside lifestyle and community areas, and the plan is simple. Turn up, swap notes on service intervals with fellow owners and maybe spot your next motor on the way out. The Autonomy also completes the group’s set-up, which looks after your car from the first test drive to every oil change and the eventual trade-in.

B Autohaus is at 78 Zone A, Vibhavadi Rangsit Frontage Rd, and is open daily 8am-5pm. For more information, call 02-938-5555.
