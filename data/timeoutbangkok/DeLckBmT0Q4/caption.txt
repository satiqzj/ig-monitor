On Saturday October 17, producer Julian Cary takes over Such A Small World at The Corner House for a talk on Black music history. He starts with work songs, songs of comfort and calling songs in churches, then follows them all the way to hip hop. Along the way, he asks why we ever sorted music by genre at all.

Afterwards, No Ref Left Club opens the floor and asks which stories, scents and sounds you’re craving right now. Grab a 🍸 at the bar, snap a few frames in the photobooth and come solo if you fancy. Plenty of people do.
