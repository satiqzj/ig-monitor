Bangkok takes ninth place among the world's friendliest cities for 2026, according to Condé Nast Traveller’s Readers’ Choice Awards.

Melbourne wins with 98.52 out of 100, followed by Porto (98.33) and Seoul (97.94). Lisbon, Amsterdam, Perth, Jaipur and Buenos Aires fill out the top eight, with  Bangkok scoring 96.16, just ahead of Cape Town.

And here, one smile can still mean 'hello', 'sorry', 'no thanks' or 'it's fine'. Efficient. 😌
