Thai fans, the wait is over. beabadoobee, aka Beatrice Laus, is coming back to Bangkok with The Powerlines Tour, her third show here since she first visited on the Beatopia tour in 2022.

This time she brings songs from Pylon, her fourth album, out since September 18. The 14 tracks start as diary entries written on the road about missing home, and the guest list includes Paramore’s Hayley Williams and Turnstile’s Brendan Yates. It follows This Is How Tomorrow Moves, which gave her a first UK number one.

Expect old favourites like ‘Glue Song’ and ‘the perfect pair’ too. Catch her at UOB Live, Emsphere on April 2, 2027.
