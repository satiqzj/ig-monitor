What if you swapped Bangkok living for something a little more Phuket? 🌴

We’re getting a first glimpse inside the completed two- and three-bedroom homes at The Standard Residences, Phuket Bang Tao – where natural light, generous outdoor spaces and relaxed interiors are designed to make everyday life feel decidedly more tropical.

And The Standard approach goes beyond the residences themselves. The brand has teamed up with Leisure Projects to create staff uniforms conceived as everyday resort wear: relaxed, functional and contemporary, with different looks designed around the people, roles and spaces they inhabit.

It’s all part of the same idea: that good design isn’t just about how a place looks, but how it feels to live in it.

From the rooms to the people bringing them to life, here’s a first look at The Standard way of living in Phuket. 🌊
