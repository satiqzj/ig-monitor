What’s it like reporting from one of the world’s most closed-off countries? 🇰🇵🎥

CNN correspondent Will Ripley (@willripleycnn) has visited North Korea 19 times – but his first assignment there came almost by chance.

Based in Tokyo at the time, Ripley was chosen ahead of more senior correspondents and sent in with an unusual brief from his boss: try to secure interviews with three Americans being detained by North Korea.

He tells Time Out how that first trip began a reporting relationship with the country that would span more than a decade.
