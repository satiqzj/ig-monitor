Doing the Bangkok Art Biennale properly usually means a lot of Grab rides and sweaty walks between venues. On Sunday November 1, City to River does the legwork for you.

From 2pm, curators walk a small group round the BAB 2026 highlights at BACC, and some of the artists come along to talk about their work. Next up is Wat Pho, where new pieces sit among the tiled chedis and the 46-metre reclining Buddha.
Around sunset, a private boat heads along the Chao Phraya and turns off at Khlong Bangkok Noi, finishing at Siri Sala Private Thai Villa for drinks and dinner.

A Curated Journey of BAB 2026: City to River is on Sun Nov 1 from 2pm. Book via @sirisala.official.
