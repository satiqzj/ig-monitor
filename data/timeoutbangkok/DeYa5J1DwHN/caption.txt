Bookworms, clear your Saturday. On October 24, the Thai Queer Book Fair takes over TK Park at centralwOrld from midday to 6pm, with tables of romance novels, poetry, fantasy, zines and handmade prints from indie publishers and printmakers. 

Stick around for panel talks, book clubs and writing workshops, catch sets from queer artists and chat face to face with the authors behind your next favourite read. The message is straightforward. These stories are for anyone who enjoys a good page-turner, whatever their gender. Bring a tote bag, because you’ll go home with more 📚 than planned.

The Thai Queer Book Fair takes place at TK Park, eighth floor, centralwOrld on Saturday, October 24, midday-6pm.
