#FINDTHELOCKERROOM is ready to be found again 🔓

@findthelockerroom.bkk, Bangkok’s most stubborn speakeasy, announced an October 23 2026 return with a new look and a new cocktail menu. The facelift was first announced in August, complete with a joke about its age: heading into its 10th year in F&B apparently makes it 30 in human years.

The Thonglor hideaway opened in 2017 and built its name on Past, Present and Future twists on classic cocktails, followed by nostalgia-soaked Time Capsule menus. Its invitation this time: “Let’s catch up.”

📍 406 Sukhumvit Soi 55, Thonglor
