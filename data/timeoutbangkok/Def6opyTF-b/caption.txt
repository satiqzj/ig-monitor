Bangkok is full of yellow flags 🟨

They mark the Jay Vegetarian Festival, with meat-free street food and temple traditions across Yaowarat and Talat Noi. Yaowarat’s festivities run until October 18 and Chow Sue Kong Shrine in Talat Noi continues until October 19.

Jay food skips meat, seafood, eggs and dairy, as well as pungent vegetables such as garlic and onions. Sad salads? Hardly. Think stir-fried noodles, crispy spring rolls, sour curry and peanut sweets. Follow the flags and come hungry.
