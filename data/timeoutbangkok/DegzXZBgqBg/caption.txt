Remember Ruby, the heartbroken character who inspired @rubys.bkk at @kimptonmaalaibangkok? Good news. She’s over it.

From October 1, the 16-seat bar next to Ms.Jigger serves the second volume of its story-led menu, timed with the hotel’s sixth birthday. Titled ‘Transformation’, it follows her as she makes fresh memories and works out who she is now. As with the first volume, each of the 13 drinks takes its name from a precious stone.

The Diamond arrives pale gold and crystal clear under a hand-cut ice pyramid. Jadeite combines Japanese whisky and matcha liqueur with yuzu, lychee and wasabi cordial, finished with gold-flecked ikura. Moonstone goes zero-proof with bread, butterscotch, balsamic vinegar and milk. Fire Opal and Sea Pearl round out the list.

Keen to try the rest? Stop by Ruby’s, open daily from 5.30pm to midnight. To book, call 02-056-9999 or email Rubys.KimptonMaaLai@ihg.com.
