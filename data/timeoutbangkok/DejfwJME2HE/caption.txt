The break from Bangkok you didn’t know you needed 😌

About 45 minutes from the city, RXV Wellness Village in Suan Sampran offers a rather more relaxing way to spend a night (or a few). 

Think 100+ treatments and activities, from traditional Thai massages and Pilates to physiotherapy and hyperbaric chamber therapy, plus cold plunges, saunas and Bor Naam’s hydrotherapy experience.

And because wellness shouldn’t mean surviving on sad salads, there’s organic, seasonal Rainbow Food and wellness teas inspired by Thai, Chinese and Ayurvedic traditions.

Bring the kids, too. There’s a wellbeing programme for them while you get your own reset.
