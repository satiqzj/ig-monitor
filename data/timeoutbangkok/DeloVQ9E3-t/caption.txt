Sra Bua translates as ‘lotus pond’, and after a stretch away the restaurant reopens at Siam Kempinski Hotel Bangkok with Chef Num Weerawat now running the kitchen.

His whole thinking rests on Thailand’s biodiversity, the sheer range of what grows and swims across the country, and it turns up on the plate as premium seasonal produce gathered from forests, farms, rivers and the coast. 

Two menus split the difference. The Signature Biodiversity Journey walks you through the full spread, while the Plants and Roots Forward Journey stays vegetable-led for anyone skipping meat and fish. Both tell the story of contemporary Thai cooking one region and one season at a time, and neither rushes you out the door.
