Grab a picnic mat and some 🦟 spray, because the Chula Open-Air Cinema Festival is back for its third edition on Friday November 6. 

Chulalongkorn University Centenary Park, just off Banthat Thong Road, puts up a big screen on its lawn. Between films there’s live 🎶 from folk singers and full bands, plus tarot readings and artists sketching on the spot. 

Fancy taking part? Applications for bands and activities close at 8pm on October 20, so head to @chulaklangplaeng for the link.
