Property launches usually mean a sales rep and a brochure. This one hands you a 🍷 instead.

Over the Chulalongkorn Day weekend (October 23-25), Lesmo Plus opens Alom Ari, its new housing project near Ari BTS, for Sip & Sound. Grab a seat at the communal tables and taste your way through more than 100 labels poured by over 10 vendors and importers, covering natural wines plus bottles from France, Italy, Chile, Thailand, Germany and Russia, a special selection from Mionetto, Mouton and executive wines from Italasia, plus the Garrison Bar with a single-malt flight tasting, then order food cooked by neighbourhood restaurants like MIA-Made In Ari, Oh! Vacola, Halfie and GASM Chocolate.

DJs rotate nightly with house, organic house and nu-disco, while art installations appear across the site once the sun sets. Wine Masterclasses and workshops fill the evening. Your pets 🐶 are warmly  welcome here. Notting beats a drink with your pets and good company. 

Online tickets cover all 3 days and include 3 tokens (B300) and a Lucaris wine glass worth B450. Early bird is B700 (first 100, until October 12), regular B800 and a team pack for five B3,500. Walk-ins pay B200 on the day for one token, glass not included.

🎟️: https://www.zipeventapp.com/e/Alom-Ari-Art-and-Wine-Fair
Sip & Sound runs 3pm-10pm at 58 Soi Suparaj 1, Samsen Nai. Parking is limited, so take the BTS.
