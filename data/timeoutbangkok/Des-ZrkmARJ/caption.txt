Bangkok’s favourite night out of the year is back 🎡✨ The Golden Mount Temple Fair returns to Wat Saket for 13 days and 13 nights of street food, fairground rides and merit-making.

Get there on opening day, November 17, for the red cloth ceremony, when devotees carry a long scarlet strip up the hill and wrap it around the chedi for good luck. Then work your way down to the market for grilled squid, khanom krok, bags of cha yen and stalls of bargains to rummage through.

📍 Wat Saket (Golden Mount), Pom Prap Sattru Phai

 📅 November 17-29
