Need an excuse to do absolutely nothing in Bangkok ☁️

Meet Quiet Room by SUBSOLUМ, a New York-style loft-inspired pop-up where plush white cushions, floor-to-ceiling windows and Bangkok’s skyline stretch out before you. Bring a book, stare dramatically into the distance or simply switch off. Newstand is popping up with drinks, too. 

📍 T-ONE Building, 39th floor, Sukhumvit 40, near BTS Thong Lo

🗓️ Until November 5 2026. Free admission; drinks charged separately.
