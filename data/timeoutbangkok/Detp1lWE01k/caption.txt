She bought a 10-class pack, struggled through the first lesson and only kept going because she’d already paid. A decade later, Kamolthip ‘O’ Tangtheintong is still at @flymetothemoon_pole_aerial in Ari – the studio she co-founded to prove pole can be a hobby, a sport and an art form all at once.
Today it runs around 300 students a week, aged 11 to 55, with dentists, lawyers and sex workers on the same poles.
