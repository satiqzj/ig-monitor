Picasso and Matisse in Bangkok for four days only 🎨 

Catch Picasso’s Nu couché et musicien (1967) and Matisse’s Femme en costume oriental (1920) at Muse: Beyond Human, alongside contemporary works and pieces from Dib Bangkok’s collection. Peihang Benoit, Natee Utarit and Dana Schutz also feature in the show, organised by Dib Bangkok with support from Christie’s, with special thanks to UOB and Bangkok University.

📍 Dib26, Sukhumvit Soi 26, Bangkok
🗓️ October 27-30 2026, 10am-7pm daily 
🎟️ Free admission
