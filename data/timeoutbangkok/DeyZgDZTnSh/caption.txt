A history-making reporting trip to Ukraine’s Snake Island almost ended in tragedy 🌊🥶

CNN’s Will Ripley (@willripleycnn) was part of the first international news crew to reach the island after Ukrainian forces recaptured it. But on the journey back, their boat became stranded – leaving the crew trapped for more than 12 hours, without communication, as temperatures plunged below freezing.

At one point, survival meant burying themselves in grass on a nearby island just to preserve body heat. ‘I never had such a strong urge… not ‘I don’t want to die’, but ‘I want to live.’’

Ripley tells Time Out about the assignment that became a fight for survival. Find the full podcast on our YouTube channel.
