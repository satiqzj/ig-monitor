Lift your spirits (and your face) in Bangkok ✨

Considering a facelift in Bangkok? Between world-class medical care, five-star hospitality and a city that knows a thing or two about looking after you, Thailand makes a compelling case for combining facial rejuvenation with a change of scenery.

From finding an experienced, board-certified surgeon to understanding your options, weighing the risks and making sure you have the right support after surgery, there’s plenty to consider before booking that flight.

That’s where Beauty Butler comes in. The Bangkok-based medical concierge helps international visitors coordinate consultations, surgeons, hospitals, accommodation, transfers and aftercare, taking some of the guesswork out of the process. A free video consultation with the surgeon also gives you a chance to discuss your goals and proposed treatment before committing to a deposit.
And while Bangkok’s rooftop bars, shopping hauls and temple-hopping will still be waiting, recovery comes first. Plan to stay around 10–14 days, follow your surgeon’s advice and give yourself time to heal before heading home.

Your face deserves some homework. Know who’s caring for you and where to turn if you have questions.
