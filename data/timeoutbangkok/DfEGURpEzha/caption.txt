Scroll through Thai social media this week and you’ll probably spot a national ID card that looks a little different. 

The newest hotspot is the registration office at Phitsanulok Municipality. Locals now queue up there in full traditional Thai dress to apply for or renew their IDs. 

Love Destiny fans tried the same trick in 2018, and this time it comes as Thailand pushes for chut thai to join UNESCO’s intangible heritage list.
