Thailand ❤️ PREP, and the feeling’s mutual. The London indie pop band are back in town for PREP Asia Tour: Live in Bangkok at Melt Livehouse Cloud 11 Hall, part of Seen Scene Space’s Maho Rasop Series.

Before the show, we sat down with them to talk about their latest release, future collaborations, how a PREP song comes together and what touring here means to them. They also had a few words for the fans who’ve stuck with them all these years.
