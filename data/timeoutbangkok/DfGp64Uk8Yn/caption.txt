Eight seats, one wooden counter and a pot of rice cooked fresh for every sitting. Meet  @sushikuuyabkk, the omakase spot on the second floor of Vivre Langsuan, run by chef Goji Kobayashi.

Goji’s family is from Fukuoka, and that’s where most of his seafood still comes from, through suppliers he knows personally. Expect seasonal snacks and cooked plates before the nigiri, plus standouts like charcoal-grilled abalone and wild bluefin tuna marinated in his own soy, served with nori from Saga.

Co-owner and certified sake sommelier Natwara Kobayashi looks after the pairings, right down to the shape of each glass.

Swipe to see the dishes up close, then plan your next omakase night. Menus start at B4,500++, and a deposit confirms your booking.

Reserve via 092-916-9299 or head to @sushikuuyabkk
