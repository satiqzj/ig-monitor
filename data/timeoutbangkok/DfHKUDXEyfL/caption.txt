Free 🎶 in the park, anyone? British acid jazz legends Incognito are heading to Benchakitti Park on Saturday November 28, with much-loved Thai singer Neung ETC. joining them on the bill. 

Expect a full horn section, big soul vocals and a few ’90s hits as the sun goes down over the skyline. Gates open at 4pm and live sets run 5pm-8pm, so bring a picnic mat, some water and mosquito spray. Entry costs nothing, but you do need to register first. Link in comments.
