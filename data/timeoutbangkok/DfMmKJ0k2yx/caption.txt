Pasta, cocktails and a park-sized view: Iris & Wild Iris (@irisandwildiris) at SO/ Bangkok pairs Italian dining with rooftop drinks above Lumphini Park, on the 29th and 30th floors overlooking North Sathorn Road.

Start at Iris, a glasshouse restaurant with art deco details, woven furnishings and plenty of green in the view. Sicilian chef Enrico Pantorno brings family traditions and a modern touch to seafood crudo and handcrafted pasta. Earlier menus featured the signature Iris lasagna and Sicilian meatballs in tomato sauce inspired by his grandmother’s recipe. Those family traditions also inspire Nonna’s Brunch, a leisurely Italian gathering with sweeping views of Lumphini Park on every Saturday noon-3pm.

After dinner, move next door to Wild Iris for cocktails and DJ sets among interiors inspired by the White Peacock. Or head upstairs to Iris Terrace, the open-air deck on the 30th floor, for sunset aperitivo or a date-night toast as the city lights come on.

Whether you’re celebrating a special occasion or looking for a memorable spot to welcome the holidays, Iris offers a little something for every kind of evening.

📍 SO/ Bangkok, 29th-30th floors, North Sathorn Road
🕔 Iris & Iris Terrace: daily, 5pm-midnight
🍸 Wild Iris: Sun-Thu 5pm-midnight; Fri-Sat 5pm-1am
