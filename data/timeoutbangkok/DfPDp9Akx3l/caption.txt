Happy Diwali!

This November, Central Department Store is celebrating Diwali with The First Light ceremony at Central Bangrak on November 3, followed by festive activities until November 4. The celebration brings together diya lamps, traditional Indian cultural performances, Indian-inspired refreshments and beautiful henna (mehndi) art.

Light a diya to welcome prosperity and seek blessings from Goddess Lakshmi, Lord Ganesha and Lord Rama. Soak up the festive atmosphere with traditional performances, enjoy Indian-inspired refreshments and let talented henna artists adorn your hands. Check @centraldepartmentstore for the ceremony time and daily activity schedule.

A little festive shopping never hurt anyone. From November 1-15, international tourists can enjoy exclusive shopping privileges, including complimentary shopping cash coupons at participating Central Department Stores, such as Central Bangrak, Central Chidlom and CENTRAL @ centralwOrld. Additional Mastercard privileges are also available, subject to terms and conditions. Browse fashion, beauty and jewellery for an early start on holiday gifts (and perhaps a little something for yourself, too).
