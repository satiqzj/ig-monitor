Looking for somewhere cool to celebrate Diwali in Bangkok? Good news. The Amazing Thailand Grand Diwali Festival 2026 takes over Quartier Avenue at EmQuartier, right off the Phrom Phong BTS, on November 26-29.

The Mall Group organises it alongside the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT), the Indian Association of Thailand and the Thai-India Tourism and Trade Association. Expect food, music, art and fashion as Bangkok’s Indian community shares its heritage with Thais, expats and tourists.

Never done Diwali before? Celebrated by Hindus, Sikhs and Jains, it’s a time for lighting clay oil lamps, drawing colourful rangoli patterns on doorsteps and passing round boxes of sweet ladoo and barfi. Buying new clothes is customary too, which explains the month-long shopping campaign.

The holiday itself falls on November 8. Until November 30, anyone who parts with B8,000 or more per day at Paragon Department Store or EM District receives a B500 M Cash coupon. A B100,000 daily total at EM District earns a B3,000 voucher.

The accompanying deal runs at Paragon Department Store and EM District until Nov 30.
