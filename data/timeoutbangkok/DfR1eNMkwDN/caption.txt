Cracked shells, sticky fingers and a quiet stand-off over the last dumpling. Sound like your kind of dinner?

From November 1, Cantonese restaurant Xian Yuan launches Treasures of the Feast, three months of hairy crab, Alaskan king crab and fresh seafood.

The eight hairy crab dishes begin with the whole crab, steamed in Chinese wine and perilla, plus three dipping sauces on the side. Shengjian bao arrive four to a plate, sesame-topped and full of roe-rich broth, so give them a minute before biting. Then come xiaolongbao and siu mai, crispy rolls that mix in Phu Phan Hae palm shoots from Sakon Nakhon, a flaky curry puff on pear sauce, egg tofu under creamy Sichuan soup, Shanghai noodles in roe sauce and claypot baked rice to finish.

King 🦀 comes more than seven ways, from garlic-steamed alongside Chinese rice cakes to wok-fried in XO sauce. Rather build your own meal? Pick Australian or Phuket 🦞, red grouper and more, then tell the kitchen how to cook it.

Swipe for a 👀 at all eight.

Treasures of the Feast runs November 1-January 31, 2027 at Xian Yuan, 5/F Dusit Central Park, which opens daily 10am-10pm (lunch sets Monday-Friday, 10am-3pm). Book on 084-237-1636 or at xianyuanbangkok.com.
