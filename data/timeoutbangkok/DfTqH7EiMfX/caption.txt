Your game plan for Friday November 6, sorted. 

52 gilded barges and 2,200 navy oarsmen row from Wasukri Pier to Wat Arun at around 2.30pm, and the BMA has 7,400 free seats waiting at six riverside sites. Rama VIII Bridge takes the biggest crowd with 4,000 chairs, while the Pinklao spot has room for just 200. 

River traffic stops at midday, so arrive early with a hat, an umbrella and something to sit on. Got navy passes from September? Don’t forget them.
