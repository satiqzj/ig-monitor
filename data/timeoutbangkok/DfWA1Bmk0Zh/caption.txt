Bangkok’s group chats get busy in December. Waldorf Astoria Bangkok answers the ‘where are we eating?’ question with five venues.

Foie gras with mulled wine jelly. Truffle and chestnut bites. A slice of stollen under the Peacock Alley’s brass art deco clock. That’s afternoon tea at Peacock Alley between November 27 and January 3, and it costs B3,200++ for two (or B5,000++ with a glass of Champagne each).

On Level 55, Bull & Bear serves A5 Bushu wagyu, 🦞 ravioli and a dessert shaped like a bauble from December 18, with centralwOrld spread out below the windows. Front Room does a Thai sharing menu built around eight flavours, while The Brasserie offers a four-course dinner on December 24 and 25, plus a New Year’s Day brunch for anyone nursing a sore head.

When the countdown starts, head one floor up to The Loft for drinks and music from 8pm (minimum spend B5,800++). Or grab a seat at Peacock Alley, where a violinist plays until the 🎆 go off over the city at midnight.
