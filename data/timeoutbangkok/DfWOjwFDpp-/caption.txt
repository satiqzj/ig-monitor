Being single finally has its financial perks 💸

The Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) is organising FREE activities for singles to explore Thailand together this Nov-Dec. The only catch? You have to be single and 20+.

From riding the Royal Blossom train to beach escapes in Phangnga, pet-friendly meetups and coffee socials in Bangkok, there's plenty of fun to be had without waiting for a plus-one.
Finally, you don't have to be the designated 'Steve' 😉
