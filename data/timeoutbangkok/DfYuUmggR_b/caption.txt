The cool season is here, so grab a 📖 and a picnic mat 🌳

Reading in the Park EP5 returns to Benjakitti Park’s Main Amphitheatre on Saturday November 21 from 3.30pm-6pm. Phones stay off the whole afternoon while hundreds of strangers settle on the lawn with a novel, then swap recommendations and chat about literature, art and culture in Thai and English.

Come from 3pm to add your current title to the ‘What are you reading?’ Post-it wall 📝
