Most Thai music fans know Thanachai Utchin as Pod, the voice of Moderndog. Fewer are aware he paints. From November 7, more than 100 of his portraits are on display at Zakke-in, one of roughly 40 sub-temples at Kyoto’s Myoshin-ji, a Zen complex founded in 1342.

His subjects are people he admires and has a real bond with, and every canvas says as much about Pod’s own mood as it does about the sitter.

The exhibition opens during Art Collaboration Kyoto (ACK 2026), which brings 63 galleries from 18 countries and regions to the city, drawing an international art crowd to Kyoto. Having collaborated with Pod on previous projects, SC is the main sponsor of his solo exhibition, reflecting its commitment to creating value for people and the world through art and culture.

If you happen to be in Kyoto in November, give it an hour of your day. The temple is a short walk from JR Hanazono Station and entry is free.
