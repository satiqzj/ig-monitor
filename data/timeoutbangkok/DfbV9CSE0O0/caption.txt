A typical test drive means a side street and a salesperson pointing out the cup holders. This one happens on a roof at One Bangkok.

MINI Go-Kart on the Roof is back from November 13-15. Missed the 2025 edition? Here’s your second shot. You pick a time, head up to the MINI Roof Track on top of the Amphitheater building at One Bangkok Park and take a MINI for a spin. The idea is to show off the ‘MINI’s ‘Go-Kart feeling’ the brand is known for (a sharp, direct, and playful driving experience) Yes, it’s a sales pitch. It’s a fun one, though.

Sessions run 4pm-10pm, Come in the evening to catch the sun dropping behind the towers, or after dark to take in the glittering cityscape while traffic crawls along Rama IV below. Lumphini MRT is a short walk away, which beats circling for parking.

Drives are booked by time slot, so sign up before you go.

🗓️November 13-15, 4pm-10pm
📍MINI Roof Track, Amphitheater, One Bangkok Park.
