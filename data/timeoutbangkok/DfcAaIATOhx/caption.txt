Auntie Moo Deng has entered the chat 🦛💕

Our favourite little chaos queen has another tiny relative to share the spotlight with. Her older brother Moo Toon and his partner Moo Manao welcomed a baby pygmy hippo on November 4 2026 at Khao Kheow Open Zoo in Chonburi.

The newest addition to the famous ‘Moo’ family is still waiting for a name and gender reveal, but one thing’s for sure: those Michelin-man rolls are already stealing hearts. Will the little one inherit Moo Deng’s famously feisty attitude, slippery sass and talent for causing chaos? Only time will tell.

So, stay tuned for the next chapter of Thailand’s favourite hippo family and what the new baby ‘Moo’ will be named.
