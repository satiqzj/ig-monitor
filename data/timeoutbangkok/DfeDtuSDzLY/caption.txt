Thailand’s biggest book fair is back in next year, and your TBR pile doesn’t stand a chance 📚

The 55thNational Book Fair and 25th Bangkok International Book Fair return for 12 full days of shopping from March 24 to April 4, 2027 at Halls 5-7, LG floor, Queen Sirikit National Convention Center.

Doors open daily 10am-9pm, and getting there is easy. Hop off the MRT at Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre station and take Exit 3.
