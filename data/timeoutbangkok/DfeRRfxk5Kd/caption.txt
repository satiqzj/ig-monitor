One person’s clear-out is another’s find of the year.
Bangkok’s vintage scene runs from blocstore’s designer archives at Lido Connect to band tees at Zotusstore and bargain heaps at Pattavikorn Market. Add Papaya Studio’s furniture and a cool-air detour to The Pantip at Ngamwongwan in Nonthaburi, and you’ve got plenty to rummage through. Collector prices or small-change finds? Pick your level of digging.
