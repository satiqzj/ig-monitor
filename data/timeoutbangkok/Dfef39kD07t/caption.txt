Who else grew up with Elmo? Good news, because he and his Sesame Street mates move in at Central Embassy this festive season, a first for Thailand.

Expect a 15-metre-plus 🎄 out front, a Sesame Street subway station on the walk to Central Chidlom (missing a train is rarely this cute) and a ball pit in Big Bird’s Nest for the kids. Yes, Cookie Monster runs a class. No, we can’t promise any 🍪 survive it.

It’s all on from November 17 to January 10, 2027.
