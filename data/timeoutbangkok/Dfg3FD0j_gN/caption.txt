Thailand’s dry days just got a little less dry. 

Under new rules that apply from November 7, five types of venue can now sell alcohol on Makha Bucha, Visakha Bucha, Asalha Bucha and the start and end of Buddhist Lent. That means international airport terminals, hotels, licensed nightspots, similar bars in designated tourist zones and large event sites. 

Your local 7-Eleven still keeps the 🍺 fridge locked, so plan ahead.
