Who grew up riding the 🎠 at Siam Park?
The roller coaster, the giant rainbow slide, the haunted house you swore you weren’t scared of. For plenty of us, ‘Suan Siam’ is where childhood weekends happened.

After floods forced a temporary closure, the Khan Na Yao favourite reopens on November 19, its birthday, to start year 47. 

Early word also points to a single pass covering 12 months of visits, with details still to be confirmed. Our ten-year-old selves would have moved in.
