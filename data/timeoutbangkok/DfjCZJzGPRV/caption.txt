Sometimes the best way to enjoy the outdoors in Bangkok might be to bring it inside 🌿

Another Yard is SUBSOLUM’s next indoor escape on the 39th floor of T-ONE Building, following its Quiet Room pop-up. This one brings green grass hills and soft mist against raw industrial architecture and Bangkok’s skyline, with drinks and light snacks from Newstand.

No hiking, no sweating, no reapplying sunscreen. Just nature in AC.

📍SUBSOLUM, 39th floor, T-ONE Building, Sukhumvit 40
🗓️November 14-December 9 2026, daily 10am-10pm
