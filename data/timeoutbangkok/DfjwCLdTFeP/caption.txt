Saints, bodies and animals share the frame with luxury goods in Jan Bican’s paintings.

Gospel of Desire, his third Bangkok show and fifth in Asia, fills the second floor of CloudD 11 with big, brightly coloured canvases. We caught up with him to talk about his latest exhibition, Gospel of Desire, and the cost of a better life.

Gospel of Desire, curated by Tereza Kotěšovská, runs at Cloud 11 ‘til January 5, 2027. It’s open 10am-8pm and entry is free.
