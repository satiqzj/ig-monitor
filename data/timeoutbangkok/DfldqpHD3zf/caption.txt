Free ⚽ on a Friday night? Yes, please. Thailand’s women’s national team take on GB Women Police in an international friendly at PAT Stadium on November 20, and you can simply turn up without a ticket or registration.

Kick-off is 5.30pm. Seats are first come, first served, so arrive early for a spot near the halfway line. Vendors sell food and drinks, and the whole family is welcome.

Parking isn’t available. Hop on the 🚇 to Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre or Khlong Toei instead.
