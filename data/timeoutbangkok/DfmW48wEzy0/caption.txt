You know when you fold down a page to mark your spot? That small crease is the starting point for JUST RIGHT CORNER: EP. 2 – Doc-Eared Club, and its call for vendors is now open.

Zine makers, indie publishers, illustrators, crafters and anyone with a shelf of old 📚 they’re ready to part with are all welcome. Stalls cover books both new and second-hand, zines and independent publications, art prints, handmade goods, vintage finds and collectibles, stationery, plus snacks and 🍹. Prefer teaching to selling? Workshop hosts can sign up too.

The ‘Doc’ stands for documenting. Following the Lost & Found project, this edition looks at what we record, what we hang on to and what we pass along. Expect crates of novels with pencilled prices, photocopied zines held together by a single staple and someone at the next table showing you how to stitch a notebook by hand.

Applications close on November 22, and successful applicants hear back by 📧 on November 23 or 24.
